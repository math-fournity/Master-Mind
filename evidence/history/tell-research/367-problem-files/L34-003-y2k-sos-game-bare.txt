## Problem

The Y2K Game is played on a \(1\times 2000\) grid.
Two players take turns writing either an \(S\) or an \(O\) in an empty square.
The first player who produces three consecutive boxes that spell \(SOS\) wins.
If all boxes are filled without producing \(SOS\), then the game is a draw.

Show that the second player has a winning strategy.
