## Problem

Let \(r>1\) be a rational number. Alice plays a solitaire game on a number line.
Initially there is a red bead at \(0\) and a blue bead at \(1\).

In one move, Alice chooses one of the beads and an integer \(k\in\mathbb Z\).
If the chosen bead is at \(x\), and the other bead is at \(y\), then the chosen bead is moved to the point \(x'\) satisfying

\[
x'-y=r^k(x-y).
\]

Find all \(r\) for which Alice can move the red bead to \(1\) in at most \(2021\) moves.
