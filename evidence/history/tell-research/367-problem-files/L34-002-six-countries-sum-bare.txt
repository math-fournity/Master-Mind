## Problem

An international society has its members from six different countries.
The list of members has \(1978\) names, numbered

\[
1,2,\ldots,1978.
\]

Prove that there is at least one member whose number is the sum of the numbers of two members from his or her own country. The two summands are allowed to be equal.
