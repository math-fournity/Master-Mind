## Problem

Let \(Q(z)\) be a complex polynomial of degree \(2026\), and suppose that all roots of \(Q\) are distinct.

Prove that there exist complex numbers \(z_1,z_2,\ldots,z_{2026}\) such that, if

\[
p_1(z)=z-z_1
\]

and, for \(2\le n\le 2026\),

\[
p_n(z)=p_{n-1}(z)^2-z_n,
\]

then \(Q(z)\) divides \(p_{2026}(z)\).
