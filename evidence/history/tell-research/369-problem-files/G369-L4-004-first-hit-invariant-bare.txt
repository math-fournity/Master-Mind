## Problem

Let

\[
0<a_1\le a_2\le a_3\le\cdots
\]

be an unbounded nondecreasing sequence of integers. For each positive integer \(n\), define \(b_n\) to be the smallest positive integer \(m\) such that

\[
a_m\ge n.
\]

Suppose that

\[
a_{37}=2026.
\]

Find the maximum possible value of

\[
a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_{37}+b_1+b_2+\cdots+b_{2026}.
\]
