## Problem

Let \(S\) be a set of integers, not necessarily positive. Suppose that:

1. There exist \(a,b\in S\) such that
   \[
   \gcd(a,b)=\gcd(a-2,b-2)=1.
   \]
2. Whenever \(x,y\in S\), possibly with \(x=y\), the integer
   \[
   x^2-y
   \]
   also belongs to \(S\).

Prove that \(S=\mathbb Z\).
