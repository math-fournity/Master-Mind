## Problem

For two distinct real numbers \(x\) and \(y\), define \(D(x,y)\) to be the unique integer \(d\) satisfying

\[
2^d\le |x-y|<2^{d+1}.
\]

Let \(F\) be a finite set of real numbers. For \(x\in F\), call a number \(d\) a scale of \(x\) in \(F\) if \(d=D(x,y)\) for some \(y\in F\), \(y\ne x\).

Let \(k\) be a positive integer. Suppose that every \(x\in F\) has at most \(k\) different scales in \(F\).

What is the largest possible value of \(|F|\)?
