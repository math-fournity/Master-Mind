## Problem

Consider the sequence \(c_1,c_2,\ldots\), where

\[
c_n=a_1^n+a_2^n+\cdots+a_8^n
\]

and \(a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_8\) are real numbers, not all equal to zero.

Suppose that infinitely many terms of the sequence \(c_n\) are equal to zero.
Find all natural numbers \(n\) such that \(c_n=0\).
