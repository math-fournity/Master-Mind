## Problem

For every positive integer \(n\), a bank issues coins of denomination \(1/n\).

Given a finite collection of such coins, not necessarily of different denominations, with total value at most

\[
99+\frac12,
\]

prove that it is possible to split this collection into \(100\) or fewer groups, such that each group has total value at most \(1\).
