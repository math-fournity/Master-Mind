There is an unknown target x among N possible candidates. A questioner may ask q adaptive yes/no questions, each of the form "does x belong to this specified subset of candidates?"

The responder is allowed two kinds of interference:
1. At most one yes/no answer may be a lie.
2. At most one answer may be the silence symbol *, meaning that no yes/no information is given in that round.

The questioner knows these rules but does not know when the lie or the silence occurs. After all questions, the questioner must output at most t candidates and must guarantee that the true target is among them.

Prove that if

N(q^2+q+1) > t(2^q+q2^{q-1}),

then no such guaranteed strategy exists.

Give a complete proof and write it to proof.md.
