Let p be a prime and let L be a subset of F_p with |L|=s. Let (A_1,B_1),...,(A_m,B_m) be ordered pairs of subsets of {1,2,...,n}. Suppose that:

1. For every i, |A_i ∩ B_i| mod p is not in L.
2. For every i<j, |A_i ∩ B_j| mod p is in L.

Prove that

m <= sum_{r=0}^s binom(n,r).

Give a complete proof and write it to proof.md.
