## Abstract

This article focuses specifically on the study of self-dual double cyclic codes over a finite field $\mathbb{F}_q$. A self-dual double cyclic code is a double cyclic code that is equal to its dual. Structurally, a double cyclic code of length $(r,s)$ over $\mathbb{F}_q$ is a $\mathbb{F}_q[x]$-submodule of $\mathbb{F}_{q,r,s}:=\mathbb{F}_q[x]/\langle x^r-1\rangle\times\mathbb{F}_q[x]/\langle x^s-1\rangle$. Moreover, any double cyclic code of length $(r,s)$ over $\mathbb{F}_q$ is generated by two pairs of polynomials in $\mathbb{F}_{q,r,s}$. From the properties of the generating elements, we provide the necessary and sufficient conditions such that two pairs of polynomials in $\mathbb{F}_{q,r,s}$ generate a self-dual code. Furthermore, we examine the existence of self-dual double cyclic codes for some specific lengths: $(r,r)$; $(r,2r)$ and $(2r,r)$; and $(r,s)$, where $\gcd(r,s)=1$. For each case, we provide a construction method with some explicit examples over various finite fields. We also observe some connections between self-dual double cyclic codes and other classes of self-dual codes.

## 1Introduction

In classical coding theory, cyclic codes are a special class of linear codes which have a strong algebraic structure that enables an efficient encoding and decoding process. The efficiency of cyclic codes makes them suitable for many practical applications that require high-speed data processing. The structure of a cyclic code of lengthnnover a finite field𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}can be identified with an ideal of the polynomial ring𝔽q​[x]/⟨xn−1⟩\mathbb{F}_{q}[x]/\langle x^{n}-1\rangle. Hence, the existence of cyclic codes of lengthnnover𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}depends on the factorization ofxn−1x^{n}-1over𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}.

Since the number of cyclic codes of a specific length is quite limited, a considerable amount of research on their generalizations has been carried out. Some of them are quasi-cyclic codes, negacyclic codes, constacyclic codes, skew-cyclic codes, and double cyclic codes. The goal is to obtain a wider variety of codes with good parameters while preserving the main structural properties. Some classes of optimal codes can be constructed using this approach.

In this article, we focus on double cyclic codes. The concept of double cyclic codes was introduced by Borges et al.[4]. In their article, they worked specifically on double cyclic codes overℤ2\mathbb{Z}_{2}. However, some of their results can easily be generalized to codes over any finite field𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}. Later, Gao et al.[8]worked on double cyclic codes overℤ4.\mathbb{Z}_{4}.Then several other works on double cyclic codes over various finite fields or finite rings have been done, such as over the finite field𝔽p\mathbb{F}_{p}[1], the finite ringℤ2+u​ℤ2\mathbb{Z}_{2}+u\mathbb{Z}_{2}, whereu2=0u^{2}=0[2], and the finite ring𝔽q+v​𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}+v\mathbb{F}_{q}, wherev2=vv^{2}=v[7]. Furthermore, the concept of double skew-cyclic codes has also been developed[3].

On the other hand, self-dual codes are another special class of linear codes which have a unique and rich mathematical structure. They have deep connections to other mathematical fields that enable the construction of codes with desirable properties. There are several studies that focus on the self-dual case of a special class of codes, such as self-dual cyclic codes[12,16], self-dual quasi-cyclic codes[14,6], self-dual negacyclic codes[10,13], and self-dual constacyclic codes[15].

Therefore, the concept of self-dual double cyclic codes is interesting to explore. In this article, we discuss some aspects of self-dual double cyclic codes over a finite field𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}, including their existence and their construction methods. We also explore some connections between self-dual double cyclic codes and other classes of self-dual codes, such as self-dual cyclic codes, self-dual quasi-cyclic codes, and self-dual negacyclic codes.

This article is organized as follows. In Section 2 we recall some basic facts regarding the double cyclic codes over𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}and their dual codes. The existence of self-dual double cyclic codes over𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}and their characterization is observed in Section 3. The last three sections discuss self-dual double cyclic codes of a specific length. In Section 4 we examine a special case of self-dual double cyclic codes of length(r,r)(r,r)over𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}. In Section 5, another special case of self-dual double cyclic codes of length(r,2​r)(r,2r)and(2​r,r)(2r,r)over𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}is examined. Then Section 6 is devoted to self-dual double cyclic codes of length(r,s)(r,s)over𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}, wheregcd⁡(r,s)=1\gcd(r,s)=1. The article ends with some concluding remarks.

In[4], Borges et al. discussed the structure and properties of double cyclic codes overℤ2\mathbb{Z}_{2}, which can be generalized to double cyclic codes over any finite field𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}. ## 3Characterization of self-dual double cyclic codes over𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}

A linear codeCCis said to be a self-dual code if it is equal to its dual, i.e.,C⟂=CC^{\perp}=C. Because of their unique and rich structure, self-dual codes become an interesting area to explore. Their deep connections to other mathematical fields such as unimodular lattices, invariant theory, and combinatorial design theory also enable the construction of codes with desirable properties.

A cyclic code that is also a self-dual code is called a self-dual cyclic code. Jia et al.[12]worked specifically on self-dual cyclic codes over a finite field𝔽q.\mathbb{F}_{q}.An important result in their article is the necessary and sufficient conditions for the existence of self-dual cyclic codes.

## Theorem 3.1([12], Section III, Theorem I).

There exists at least one self-dual cyclic code of lengthnnover𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}if and only ifqqis a power of 2 andnnis even.

Note that ifqqis a power of2,2,then for any even positive integernn, the cyclic code generated byxn/2+1x^{n/2}+1is self-dual of lengthnnover𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}. This code is called the trivial self-dual cyclic code. In[12], it was also shown that for some even positive integernn, a nontrivial self-dual cyclic code of lengthnnover𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}exists, but for some othernn, only the trivial one exists.

In this section, we discuss self-dual double cyclic codes, i.e. double cyclic codes which are also self-dual codes. For the separable case, the following theorem is a simple extension from Theorem3.1.

## Theorem 3.2.

## 7Concluding remark

In this article, we have investigated some properties of self-dual double cyclic codes over a finite field𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}.
Among our main contributions are the necessary and sufficient conditions for the polynomials in the generating set of self-dual double cyclic codes. This characterization works for any length(r,s)(r,s)and over any finite field𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q}.

We also observe some special cases of self-dual double cyclic codes of a specific length. In each case, we characterize the existence of such codes, which mainly depends on the polynomial factorization over finite fields. We also find that in some cases, self-dual double cyclic codes only exist over some specific finite fields. Based on the characterizations, we formalize an explicit construction method and obtain some explicit examples. Moreover, some of the examples are optimal self-dual codes.

We also find that some cases are related to other classes of codes. Self-dual double cyclic codes of length(r,r)(r,r)are equivalent to self-dual 2-quasi-cyclic codes, while self-dual double cyclic codes of length(r,2​r)(r,2r)and(2​r,r)(2r,r)are related to self-dual negacyclic codes. Therefore, it is theoretically possible to apply our results to construct other classes of codes.
