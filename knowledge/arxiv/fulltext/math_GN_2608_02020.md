## 1Introduction

Compactness ranks among the most vital properties in general topology and has been widely investigated and thoroughly studied by topologists (see[1]). Actually, a topological spaceXXis compact if and only if every directed open cover ofXXcontainsXX. In Domain theory, for a posetPP, every directedDDofPPis always irreducible with respect to the Scott topology. Irreducible sets are an important class of sets for investigating the sobriety ofT0T_{0}spaces in Domain theory. In[15], Zhao and Ho introduced SI-compactness and essentially, they generalized directed open covers to Scott irreducible open covers. As we know, irreducible sets is strictly weaker than directed sets. For instance, for the dcpo𝒥\mathcal{J}constructed by Johnstone,𝒥\mathcal{J}is irreducible but not directed (see[7]). For more examples of irreducible sets that are not directed, we refer the reader to[9,8,16]. Accordingly, SI-compactness is stronger than compactness. In[15], Zhao and Ho raised the following two problems:

(1) Is there a compact space which is notS​ISI-compact?

(2) Is the product of twoS​ISI-compact spaces always SI-compact?
In[4], the authors constructed a uncountable compact sober space which is notS​ISI-compact by using the complete lattice constructed by Isbell (see[6]). Recently, it has proved that the countable Scott non-sober complete lattice constructed by Miao, Xi, Li and Zhao[10]endowed with the lower topology is a countable compact and nonS​ISI-compact space (see[5]). Furthermore, it has proved that every topological space is a dense open subspace of aS​ISI-compact space and the closed subspace of aS​ISI-compact space is stillS​ISI-compact (see[5]). Thus, the first problem has a positive solution. In section 3, we show that the product of finitely manyS​ISI-compact space is stillS​ISI-compact, thus the second question has also been resolved.

Reflective subcategories of the category ofT0T_{0}spaces represent an important research focus in Domain theory. The categories ofdd-spaces, well-filtered spaces and sober spaces, three known classes of spaces, are all full reflective subcategories of𝐓𝐨𝐩0\mathbf{Top}_{0}(see[13,2,12]). For other reflective subcategories of the category ofT0T_{0}spaces, we refer the reader to[14,11].
Naturally, it arises the following question:

Is the category of allS​ISI-compact spaces a reflective full subcategory of𝐓𝐨𝐩\mathbf{Top}?

In section 4, we give a negative answer for the above question. Specifically, we verify that theS​ISI-compact reflection of the discrete spaceℕ\mathbb{N}does not exist.
