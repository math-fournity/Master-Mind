## Abstract

For a finite CW pair $(K,L)$, we show how to construct a Poincaré triad $(P;\partial_0 P,\partial_1 P)$ and a weak homotopy equivalence $(K,L) \to (P,\partial_0 P)$. Furthermore, the Poincaré triad has a trivial Spivak normal fibration. The proof is homotopy-theoretic.

## 1.Introduction

The notion of a manifold thickening of a finite complex was invented by Mazur[12]and was further developed by Wall[14]. The idea is that one can replace the cells of a finite complexKKinductively by handles provided that the geometric dimension of the handles is sufficiently large.
The result is a compact smooth manifoldMMwith boundary having the homotopy type ofKK. Then heuristically,MMis a “regular neighborhood” ofKK. If the dimension is sufficiently large, then the set of concordance classes
of manifold thickenings ofKKis in bijection with[K,B​O][K,BO], the abelian group of isomorphism classes of
stable vector bundles overKK[14, prop. 5.1].

In a recent manuscript[8], it was necessary to deal with the analogous notion ofPoincaré thickening.Roughly speaking, the latter consists of a Poincaré pair(P,∂P)(P,\partial P)together with a choice of homotopy equivalenceK≃PK\simeq P.
The collection of Poincaré thickenings of a given dimensionddis equipped with an equivalence relation, concordance, and the resulting set of equivalence classes is
denoted by𝒯d​(K).\mathscr{T}_{d}(K)\,.

There is also a stabilization mapσ:𝒯d​(K)→𝒯d+1​(K)\sigma\colon\mathscr{T}_{d}(K)\to\mathscr{T}_{d+1}(K)which is induced by taking cartesian product with the unit interval.
If we let𝒯∞​(K)\mathscr{T}_{\infty}(K)denote the colimit
with respect to stabilization, there is a free and transitive action[K,B​G]×𝒯∞​(K)→𝒯∞​(K),[K,BG]\times\mathscr{T}_{\infty}(K)\to\mathscr{T}_{\infty}(K)\,,

where[K,B​G][K,BG]is the abelian group of fiber homotopy types of stable spherical fibrations overKK(compare[12, p. 79]in the smooth case).
The action is defined by twisted suspension with respect to a spherical fibration.

Furthermore,𝒯∞​(K)\mathscr{T}_{\infty}(K)is equipped with a preferred basepoint
which is represented by any Poincaré thickening(P,∂P)(P,\partial P)having trivial Spivak normal fibration.
We call any such thickening aEuclidean Poincaré thickening. It is unique up to stable concordance.
As the action is free and transitive, the orbit of the basepoint defines a bijection𝒯∞​(K)≅[K,B​G].\mathscr{T}_{\infty}(K)\cong[K,BG]\,.

In particular, to every stable thickening ofKKthere is a corresponding homotopy classK→B​GK\to BG; call this theclassifying mapof the thickening.
If one is willing to appeal to manifolds, then
Spivak’s paper[13]provides a construction of a Euclidean thickening.
In[5]we gave an alternative homotopy-theoretic construction.
The goal of this paper is torelativizethe result.
