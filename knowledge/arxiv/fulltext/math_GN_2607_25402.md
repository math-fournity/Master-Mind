## Abstract

Let \(N\) be a closed normal subgroup of a topological group \(G\), and let \(\widehat q:ρG\toρ(G/N)\) extend the quotient homomorphism. We prove that \(r_ρ(G/N)=\widehat q\bigl(r_ρ(G)\bigr)\) holds if and only if \(\widehat q\) is onto and \(\widehat q^{-1}(G/N)=G\), and we show that neither condition implies the other. Both conditions hold whenever \(N\) is Raikov complete and almost metrizable. Consequently, pseudocompactness of \(r_ρ(G)\) passes to \(r_ρ(G/N)\). In particular, the conclusion applies to closed locally compact normal subgroups.

## 1Introduction

The study of remainders of topological groups is a well-established part of general topology. Comfort and Ross studied pseudocompactness in topological groups[12]. Arhangel’skii proved that, for every Hausdorff compactificationb​GbGof a topological groupGG, the remainderb​G∖GbG\setminus Gis either pseudocompact or Lindelöf[3]. Subsequent work addressed normality, first countability, covering properties, and metrizability of compactification remainders[2,4,8,9]. Arhangel’skii and Choban studied group-remainders, including the Rajkov remainder[5], and later considered completeness-type properties, products, and perfect images of group-remainders[6,7].

For a topological groupGG, letρ​G\rho Gdenote its Raikov completion and putrρ​(G)=ρ​G∖G.r_{\rho}(G)=\rho G\setminus G.

This is the Raikov remainder ofGG. It is a completion remainder and need not be a compactification remainder. We study its behaviour under the quotient homomorphismq:G⟶G/N,q:G\longrightarrow G/N,

whereNNis a closed normal subgroup ofGG. The homomorphismqqextends uniquely toq^:ρ​G⟶ρ​(G/N).\widehat{q}:\rho G\longrightarrow\rho(G/N).

Althoughqqis onto,q^\widehat{q}need not be onto. Even when it is onto, a point ofrρ​(G)r_{\rho}(G)may be mapped intoG/NG/N. These are the two obstructions to the quotient-remainder identity.

Our first quotient result gives an exact criterion. For an arbitrary closed normal subgroupNN,rρ​(G/N)=q^​(rρ​(G))r_{\rho}(G/N)=\widehat{q}\bigl(r_{\rho}(G)\bigr)

holds precisely whenq^​(ρ​G)=ρ​(G/N)andq^−1​(G/N)=G.\widehat{q}(\rho G)=\rho(G/N)\quad\hbox{and}\quad\widehat{q}^{-1}(G/N)=G.

The examples in the final subsection show that neither condition implies the other. Thus surjectivity of the map between completions and the inverse-image condition are distinct requirements.

The main theorem verifies both requirements whenNNis Raikov complete and almost metrizable. In this caseq^\widehat{q}is a quotient homomorphism with kernelNN,q^−1​(G/N)=G,\widehat{q}^{-1}(G/N)=G,

andrρ​(G/N)=q^​(rρ​(G)).r_{\rho}(G/N)=\widehat{q}\bigl(r_{\rho}(G)\bigr).

It follows that pseudocompactness ofrρ​(G)r_{\rho}(G)passes torρ​(G/N)r_{\rho}(G/N). Closed locally compact normal subgroups form an important special case.

The proof separates the remainder criterion from the completeness of the quotient(ρ​G)/N(\rho G)/N. Related completion facts occur in the proof of[18, Theorem 4.5]. For almost metrizable kernels, the required quotient completeness follows from Leischner’s theorem on neutral subgroups[17]. The relevant completion results are discussed before Proposition3.12and Lemma3.13.

For comparison, we also give formulas for closed subgroups and finite products and derive the corresponding preservation results. Section 2 fixes the notation and the preservation facts used later. Section 3 treats closed subgroups, finite products, quotient homomorphisms, the general completion criterion, Raikov-complete almost metrizable kernels, applications, and sharpness questions.

## 3.5Raikov-complete almost metrizable kernels

For almost metrizable kernels, the remaining hypothesis follows from known quotient-completeness results. No precompactness assumption is imposed onGG, and the kernel need not be compact.

Leischner’s theorem concerns neutral subgroups and completeness for the supremum of the induced left and right quotient uniformities. The next lemma applies it to normal almost metrizable kernels, together with the characterization of Čech-complete almost metrizable groups. Quotients by arbitrary closed normal subgroups need not be Raikov complete[16,17]. Related local properties of quotient maps by locally compact subgroups were studied by Arhangel’skii[1].

## Lemma 3.13.

LetLLbe a Raikov-complete topological group, and letKKbe a closed normal subgroup ofLLthat is both Raikov complete and almost metrizable. ThenL/KL/Kis Raikov complete.

## Proof.

SinceKKis almost metrizable and Raikov complete, it is Čech-complete[10, Theorem 4.3.15]. As a normal subgroup,KKis neutral. Leischner’s theorem shows thatL/KL/Kis complete for the supremum of the induced left and right quotient uniformities[17, statement (***)]. BecauseKKis normal, this supremum is the two-sided uniformity ofL/KL/K. HenceL/KL/Kis Raikov complete.
∎

By[19, Lemma 13.13, p. 242], every locally compact topological group is almost metrizable in the sense used here. Thus locally compact kernels satisfy the almost-metrizability hypothesis in Lemma3.13. We shall also use the following standard completeness theorem.

## Lemma 3.14([10, Theorem 3.6.24]).

Every locally compact topological group is Raikov complete.

Quotient completeness now follows from the preceding lemmas. Propositions3.12and3.9then give the following theorem on Raikov remainders.

In particular, ifrρ​(G)r_{\rho}(G)is pseudocompact, thenrρ​(G/N)r_{\rho}(G/N)is pseudocompact.

## Proof.

By Lemma3.10, applied withL=ρ​GL=\rho G, the subgroupNNis closed and normal inρ​G\rho G. The topology and the two-sided uniformity onNNinduced fromρ​G\rho Gagree with those induced fromGG. HenceNNremains Raikov complete and almost metrizable. Lemma3.13shows that(ρ​G)/N(\rho G)/Nis Raikov complete. Proposition3.12shows thatq^\widehat{q}is a quotient homomorphism with kernelNNand gives the inverse-image equality and the remainder formula. The final assertion follows from Lemma2.3.
∎

## Corollary 3.16.

LetGGbe a topological group, letNNbe a closed locally compact normal subgroup ofGG, and letq^:ρ​G→ρ​(G/N)\widehat{q}:\rho G\to\rho(G/N)

be the extension of the quotient homomorphismq:G→G/Nq:G\to G/N. Thenq^\widehat{q}is a quotient homomorphism with kernelNN,q^−1​(G/N)=G,\widehat{q}^{-1}(G/N)=G,

andrρ​(G/N)=q^​(rρ​(G)).r_{\rho}(G/N)=\widehat{q}\bigl(r_{\rho}(G)\bigr).

In particular, ifrρ​(G)r_{\rho}(G)is pseudocompact, thenrρ​(G/N)r_{\rho}(G/N)is pseudocompact.

## Proof.

By Lemma3.14, the groupNNis Raikov complete. It is also almost metrizable[19, Lemma 13.13, p. 242]. The conclusion follows from Theorem3.15.
∎

## Corollary 3.17.

LetGGbe a topological group, and letNNbe a closed locally compact normal subgroup ofGG. Ifrρ​(G)r_{\rho}(G)is compact or countably compact, thenrρ​(G/N)r_{\rho}(G/N)has the same property.

## Proof.

By Corollary3.16,rρ​(G/N)=q^​(rρ​(G)).r_{\rho}(G/N)=\widehat{q}(r_{\rho}(G)).

The conclusion follows because compactness and countable compactness are preserved by continuous images.
∎

## 3.6Applications and examples

The theorem can also be stated in exact-sequence form.

## Corollary 3.18.

Let1⟶N⟶G⟶qQ⟶11\longrightarrow N\longrightarrow G\stackrel{{\scriptstyle q}}{{\longrightarrow}}Q\longrightarrow 1

be an exact sequence of topological groups, whereqqis a quotient homomorphism andNNis a closed normal subgroup ofGG. Suppose thatNNis Raikov complete and almost metrizable. In particular, ifrρ​(G)r_{\rho}(G)is pseudocompact, thenrρ​(Q)r_{\rho}(Q)is pseudocompact.

## Proof.

LetqN:G→G/Nq_{N}:G\to G/Nbe the quotient homomorphism, and writeq^N:ρ​G→ρ​(G/N)\widehat{q}_{N}:\rho G\to\rho(G/N)for its extension. The exact-sequence formulation includes split projections. LetHHbe a topological group, letLLbe a locally compact topological group, and letq:H×L⟶Hq:H\times L\longrightarrow H

be the projection onto the first factor. Letq^:ρ​(H×L)⟶ρ​H\widehat{q}:\rho(H\times L)\longrightarrow\rho H

## 3.7Sharpness and open problems

The quotient-completeness hypothesis in Proposition3.12cannot be omitted. Quotients of Raikov-complete groups need not be Raikov complete[16].

## Proposition 3.19.

LetLLbe a Raikov-complete topological group, letNNbe a closed normal subgroup ofLL, and letq:L→L/Nq:L\to L/Nbe the quotient homomorphism. IfL/NL/Nis not Raikov complete, then the extensionq^:ρ​L→ρ​(L/N)\widehat{q}:\rho L\to\rho(L/N)

ofqqis not onto, whileq^−1​(L/N)=ρ​L=L.\widehat{q}^{-1}(L/N)=\rho L=L.

## Proof.

SinceLLis Raikov complete, we regardρ​L\rho LasLL. For everyx∈ρ​L=Lx\in\rho L=L, the valueq^​(x)=q​(x)\widehat{q}(x)=q(x)belongs toL/NL/N. Henceq^−1​(L/N)=ρ​L=L\widehat{q}^{-1}(L/N)=\rho L=L. Ifq^\widehat{q}were onto, thenρ​(L/N)=q^​(ρ​L)=q​(L)=L/N,\rho(L/N)=\widehat{q}(\rho L)=q(L)=L/N,

contrary to the assumption thatL/NL/Nis not Raikov complete.
∎

Thus surjectivity ofq^:ρ​L→ρ​(L/N)\widehat{q}:\rho L\to\rho(L/N)

can fail even when the preimage condition holds. In particular,q^\widehat{q}need not be onto even thoughq:L→L/Nq:L\to L/Nis onto.

## Corollary 3.20.

LetLL,NN, andqqbe as in Proposition3.19. Thenrρ​(L)=∅,r_{\rho}(L)=\varnothing,

whereasrρ​(L/N)≠∅.r_{\rho}(L/N)\neq\varnothing.

Moreover,q^​(rρ​(L))=∅.\widehat{q}\bigl(r_{\rho}(L)\bigr)=\varnothing.

Consequently,rρ​(L/N)≠q^​(rρ​(L)).r_{\rho}(L/N)\neq\widehat{q}\bigl(r_{\rho}(L)\bigr).

## Proof.

SinceLLis Raikov complete,rρ​(L)=∅r_{\rho}(L)=\varnothing. SinceL/NL/Nis not Raikov complete, its Raikov completion is a proper completion and thereforerρ​(L/N)≠∅r_{\rho}(L/N)\neq\varnothing. Finally,q^​(rρ​(L))=q^​(∅)=∅\widehat{q}(r_{\rho}(L))=\widehat{q}(\varnothing)=\varnothing, so the remainder formula fails.
∎

This does not yet give a counterexample to preservation of pseudocompactness under quotients. The original remainder is empty and hence pseudocompact, but the argument shows only that the quotient remainder is nonempty, not that it fails to be pseudocompact.

The next example separates surjectivity from the preimage condition of Proposition3.9.

## Example 3.21.

Letppbe a prime and letG=ℤG=\mathbb{Z}be the additive group of integers with thepp-adic topology. Then the Raikov completion ofGGis the additive groupℤp\mathbb{Z}_{p}ofpp-adic integers. Fix a positive integerkkand putN=pk​ℤ.N=p^{k}\mathbb{Z}.

The subgroupNNis closed and normal inGG, and it is almost metrizable because it is metrizable. It is not Raikov complete, since its Raikov completion ispk​ℤpp^{k}\mathbb{Z}_{p}.

The quotientG/NG/Nis the finite discrete groupℤ/pk​ℤ\mathbb{Z}/p^{k}\mathbb{Z}, and henceρ​(G/N)=G/N.\rho(G/N)=G/N.

The extension of the quotient homomorphism is the natural mapq^:ℤp→ℤ/pk​ℤ.\widehat{q}:\mathbb{Z}_{p}\to\mathbb{Z}/p^{k}\mathbb{Z}.

This map is onto. Sinceρ​(G/N)=G/N\rho(G/N)=G/N, we haveq^−1​(G/N)=ℤp≠ℤ=G.\widehat{q}^{-1}(G/N)=\mathbb{Z}_{p}\neq\mathbb{Z}=G.

Thus the preimage condition in Proposition3.9is not automatic, even when the induced map on completions is onto.

Each fibrea+pk​ℤpa+p^{k}\mathbb{Z}_{p}ofq^\widehat{q}, wherea∈ℤa\in\mathbb{Z}, contains points outsideℤ\mathbb{Z}, becausea+pk​ℤa+p^{k}\mathbb{Z}is a proper dense subset ofa+pk​ℤpa+p^{k}\mathbb{Z}_{p}. Thereforeq^​(rρ​(G))=G/N.\widehat{q}(r_{\rho}(G))=G/N.

On the other hand,rρ​(G/N)=∅.r_{\rho}(G/N)=\varnothing.

Hence the image formula can fail when the kernel is almost metrizable but not Raikov complete. Theorem3.15does not apply becauseNNis not Raikov complete.

Proposition3.19shows that surjectivity may fail while the preimage condition holds, whereas Example3.21shows that the preimage condition may fail while surjectivity holds. Thus neither condition in Proposition3.9follows from the other under the standing assumptions. The first question concerns quotient completeness beyond the almost metrizable case. BecauseNNis normal and hence neutral, Leischner’s inframetrizable quotient theorem gives completeness of(ρ​G)/N(\rho G)/Nfor the supremum of the induced left and right quotient uniformities whenever the ambient Raikov-complete groupρ​G\rho Gis inframetrizable. SinceNNis normal, this supremum is the two-sided uniformity of the quotient group. Thus(ρ​G)/N(\rho G)/Nis Raikov complete in this case[17]. The next two questions concern preservation of pseudocompactness under quotient homomorphisms.

## Question 3.22.

LetNNbe a closed normal subgroup of a topological groupGG, and suppose thatNNis Raikov complete but not almost metrizable. Outside the case whereρ​G\rho Gis inframetrizable, what additional conditions onρ​G\rho GandNNensure that(ρ​G)/N(\rho G)/Nis Raikov complete?

## Question 3.23.

Does there exist a topological groupGGand a closed normal subgroupNNsuch thatrρ​(G)r_{\rho}(G)is pseudocompact butrρ​(G/N)r_{\rho}(G/N)is not pseudocompact?

## Question 3.24.

LetGGbe a topological group such thatrρ​(G)r_{\rho}(G)is normal as a topological space and pseudocompact. For which closed normal subgroupsNNofGGmustrρ​(G/N)r_{\rho}(G/N)be pseudocompact?

An affirmative answer to Question3.23would show that pseudocompactness of Raikov remainders is not preserved by arbitrary quotient homomorphisms. A negative answer would establish such preservation for every closed normal subgroup, without any almost-metrizability assumption on the kernel.
