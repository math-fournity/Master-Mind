## Abstract

Let $(\mathcal{A},G,α)$ be a $C^*$-dynamical system. Unique extension properties for the $C^*$-inclusion $\mathcal{A} \subseteq \mathcal{A} \rtimes_{α,r} G$ have been studied extensively. In this paper, we investigate unique extension properties for some other natural ``dynamical'' $C^*$-inclusions, namely:   $\bullet$ $C_r^*(G) \subseteq \mathcal{A} \rtimes_{α,r} G$;   $\bullet$ $\mathcal{A}^G \subseteq \mathcal{A}$, where $\mathcal{A}^G$ is the fixed-point subalgebra;   $\bullet$ $Z(\mathcal{A})^c \subseteq \mathcal{A} \rtimes_{α,r} G$, where $Z(\mathcal{A})^c = Z(\mathcal{A})' \cap (\mathcal{A} \rtimes_{α,r} G)$ is the relative commutant of the center.   Our hope is that a larger selection of examples will enable progress on some lingering open problems, in particular (1) the relationship between aperiodicity and the unique pseudo-expectation property and (2) the relationship between the faithful unique pseudo-expectation property and norming.

## 1.Introduction

Let(𝒜,G,α)(\operatorname{\mathcal{A}},G,\alpha)be aC∗C^{*}-dynamical system. Unique extension properties for theC∗C^{*}-inclusion𝒜⊆𝒜⋊α,rG\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\subseteq\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\rtimes_{\alpha,r}Ghave been studied extensively, and are now completely understood. Indeed, we have the following summary result (see Section1.1below for any unfamiliar notation or terminology):

## Theorem 1.1.

Let(𝒜,G,α)(\operatorname{\mathcal{A}},G,\alpha)be aC∗C^{*}-dynamical system. Then the following statements hold:
- i.

𝒜⊆𝒜⋊α,rG\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\subseteq\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\rtimes_{\alpha,r}Ghas thepure extension property(PEP)⇔\iffα^:G↷𝒜^\hat{\alpha}:G\curvearrowright\mathcal{\widehat{A}}is pointwise free.[49, Corollary 2.5]
- ii.

𝒜⊆𝒜⋊α,rG\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\subseteq\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\rtimes_{\alpha,r}Ghas thealmost extension property(AEP)⇔\iffα^:G↷𝒜^\hat{\alpha}:G\curvearrowright\mathcal{\widehat{A}}is essentially free (i.e., pointwise free on a dense set).[49, Corollary 2.6]
- iii.

𝒜⊆𝒜⋊α,rG\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\subseteq\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\rtimes_{\alpha,r}Ghas aunique pseudo-expectation⇔\iffα:G↷𝒜\alpha:G\curvearrowright\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}is pointwise properly outer⇔\iff𝒜⊆𝒜⋊α,rG\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\subseteq\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\rtimes_{\alpha,r}Gis aperiodic.[48, Theorem 3.5]and[32, Proposition 2.4]
- iv.

𝒜⊆𝒜⋊α,rG\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\subseteq\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\rtimes_{\alpha,r}Ghas aunique conditional expectation⇔\iffα:G↷𝒜\alpha:G\curvearrowright\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}is pointwise freely acting⇔\iff𝒜′∩(𝒜⋊α,rG)=Z​(𝒜)\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}^{\prime}\cap(\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\rtimes_{\alpha,r}G)=Z(\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}).[48, Theorem 3.2]

Of course the sameC∗C^{*}-dynamical system(𝒜,G,α)(\operatorname{\mathcal{A}},G,\alpha)gives rise to other importantC∗C^{*}-inclusions, for exampleCr∗​(G)⊆𝒜⋊α,rGC_{r}^{*}(G)\subseteq\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\rtimes_{\alpha,r}Gand𝒜G⊆𝒜\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}^{G}\subseteq\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}, which can exhibit very different behavior than𝒜⊆𝒜⋊α,rG\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\subseteq\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\rtimes_{\alpha,r}G. These less-studied dynamicalC∗C^{*}-inclusions have received more attention lately[1,13,42,51], and the purpose of this paper is to investigate their unique extension properties, in particular to determine when they have a unique pseudo-expectation. Intrinsic interest notwithstanding, our main motivation is to increase the available supply ofC∗C^{*}-inclusions with the unique pseudo-expectation property, with an eye toward making progress on some fundamental open problems, such as the relationship between the unique pseudo-expectation property and aperiodicity (see Section1.2below), as well as the connection between the faithful unique pseudo-expectation property and norming[40].


In the remainder of Section1, we establish notation and discuss some preliminaries. In Section2, we prove some unique pseudo-expectation results forC∗C^{*}-inclusions which are not necessarily dynamical in nature. In particular we consider finite-dimensional inclusions, finite-index inclusions (in the sense of Watatani), and corner inclusions. These more general results facilitate our analysis of dynamicalC∗C^{*}-inclusions in Section3, where the reader will find our main results. There we consider
- •

the inclusionCr∗​(G)⊆𝒜⋊α,rGC_{r}^{*}(G)\subseteq\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\rtimes_{\alpha,r}G, for which uniqueness of pseudo-expectations is related to unique ergodicity ofα:G↷𝒜\alpha:G\curvearrowright\operatorname{\mathcal{A}};
- •

the inclusion𝒜G⊆𝒜\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}^{G}\subseteq\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}for|G|<∞|G|<\infty, which is a corner of the inclusion𝒜⋊α,rG⊆𝒜⊗B​(ℓ2​(G))\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\rtimes_{\alpha,r}G\subseteq\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\otimes B(\ell^{2}(G)), for which Watatani’s theory of finite-index inclusions is an incisive tool;
- •

and the inclusionZ​(𝒜)c⊆𝒜⋊α,rGZ(\operatorname{\mathcal{A}})^{c}\subseteq\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\rtimes_{\alpha,r}G, whereZ​(𝒜)cZ(\operatorname{\mathcal{A}})^{c}(the relative commutant of the center) serves as a technically less onerous proxy forC∗​(𝒜∪𝒜c)C^{*}(\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}\cup\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}^{c})(theC∗C^{*}-algebra generated by𝒜\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}and its relative commutant𝒜c\operatorname{\mathcal{A}}^{c}).

Although we make good progress in each of the aforementioned directions, there is still substantial room for our results to be strengthened and/or generalized, and the open problems mentioned at the end of the previous paragraph remain unsolved.
