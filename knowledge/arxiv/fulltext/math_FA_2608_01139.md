## Abstract.

The present work presents a real Paley-Wiener Theorem within the space of
multi-anisotropic ultradifferentiable functions. We also show new properties
of this space.

## 1.Introduction

The multi-anisotropic Gevrey spaceℰs,Γ​(Ω),\mathcal{E}^{s,\Gamma}(\Omega),whose
definition was given explicitly in the mathematical literature by Luisa
Zanghirati in the eighties of the last century in the paper[21], has
been the subject of many works and applications. This space enters the class
of the so-called spaces of ultradifferentiable functions considered in
different fields of Mathematics as extensions or generalisations of the
classical space of real analytic functions𝒜​(Ω).\mathcal{A}(\Omega).A
well-known one of them that also interest us in this paper is the
Denjoy-Carleman spaceℰM​(Ω),\mathcal{E}^{M}\left(\Omega\right),see[11],[13],[16]. The Beurling spaceℰω​(Ω)\mathcal{E}^{\omega}\left(\Omega\right)in the sense of[8], see[1]and[2], can be considered as an other facet of the Denjoy-Carleman
space, but not a duplicata. As more general than the spaceℰs,Γ​(Ω),\mathcal{E}^{s,\Gamma}(\Omega),is the inhomogeneous Gevrey spaceℰs,λ​(Ω)\mathcal{E}^{s,\lambda}(\Omega)of[17], see[19], defined in the spirit
of the space𝒞L​(Ω)\mathcal{C}^{L}(\Omega)studied by L. Hörmander, see[16].

Recall that the spaceℰs,Γ​(Ω)\mathcal{E}^{s,\Gamma}(\Omega)has its defining
parameters the weight sequence(k!s)k(k!^{s})_{k}and a regular Newton
polyhedronΓ,\Gamma,and has its particular case the classical anisotropic
Gevrey spaceℰs,(l1,…,ln)​(Ω)\mathcal{E}^{s,\left(l_{1},...,l_{n}\right)}(\Omega)due
to M. Gevrey[14]. The spaceℰM​(Ω)\mathcal{E}^{M}\left(\Omega\right)is defined with the help of a weight sequenceMMand the spaceℰω​(Ω)\mathcal{E}^{\omega}\left(\Omega\right)is defined with the help of a weight
functionω.\omega.While the spaceℰs,λ​(Ω)\mathcal{E}^{s,\lambda}(\Omega)depends on the weight sequence(k!s)k(k!^{s})_{k}and an other type of weight
functionλ.\lambda.

The study of the relationship between these cited spaces is a natural
question. The paper[3]responded to this issue by finding the
relationship between the spacesℰM​(Ω)\mathcal{E}^{M}\left(\Omega\right)andℰω​(Ω),\mathcal{E}^{\omega}\left(\Omega\right),while the work[10]targeted the spacesℰω​(Ω)\mathcal{E}^{\omega}\left(\Omega\right)andℰs,λ​(Ω).\mathcal{E}^{s,\lambda}(\Omega).The paper[4]gives an
answer to this question for the spacesℰM​(Ω)\mathcal{E}^{M}\left(\Omega\right)andℰs,Γ​(Ω),\mathcal{E}^{s,\Gamma}(\Omega),it also introduced a class of
ultradifferentiable functions, depending on a weight sequenceMMand a
regular Newton polyhedronΓ,\Gamma,unifying both spaces, denoted byℰM,Γ​(Ω)\mathcal{E}^{M,\Gamma}\left(\Omega\right)and called the space of
multi-anisotropic ultradifferentiable functions.

A Fourier analysis and even a micro-local analysis with respect to theℰM\mathcal{E}^{M}-regularity is well-known, see e.g. Hörmander’s book[16]and the references therein. What about the classℰs,Γ​(Ω)\mathcal{E}^{s,\Gamma}(\Omega)very few is done. Indeed, a first work on a
micro-local analysis with respect to the classical anisotropic Gevrey space
is done by L. Zanghirati in[22], then an attempt to go further to
study theℰs,Γ\mathcal{E}^{s,\Gamma}-micro-regularity in the framework of
distributions is proposed by the paper[12]following the approach
proposed within inhomogeneous Gevrey classes.

So, this paper has as its aim to present a first step for a Fourier analysis
related to theℰM,Γ\mathcal{E}^{M,\Gamma}-regularity paving the way to aℰM,Γ\mathcal{E}^{M,\Gamma}-micro-local analysis which will be done in a
forthcoming work. We prove a Paley-Wiener type Theorem within the space of
multi-anisotropic ultradifferentiable functions. We also show new properties
of the spaceℰM,Γ​(Ω).\mathcal{E}^{M,\Gamma}\left(\Omega\right).

The paper is organised as follows. The second section is devoted to recall
classical spaces of ultradifferentiable functions such the Denjoy-Carleman
space and the multi-anisotropic Gevrey space. The third section presents the
space of multi-anisotropic ultradifferentiable functions and some new
properties. In the last section a Paley-Wiener type Theorem for the spaceℰM,Γ​(Ω)\mathcal{E}^{M,\Gamma}\left(\Omega\right)is proved, as a consequence
of this result we compare, in the sense of inclusion, the spaces of
multi-anisotropic ultradifferentiable functions.
