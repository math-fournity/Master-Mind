## Abstract

In this paper, we obtain the structure of a class of contractive projections, known as generalized tri-circular projections, on some functional Banach spaces on the open unit disk $\mathbb{D}$, which includes Hardy, Bergman and Bloch spaces. We then consider the space $S^p_{\mathcal{K}}$ of $\mathcal{K}$-valued analytic functions on $\mathbb{D}$ such that $f' \in H^p(\mathcal{K})$ and give complete description of generalized tri-circular projections on this space. Here, $\mathcal{K}$ is a complex separable Hilbert space, and $H^p(\mathcal{K})$ denotes the Hardy space of $\mathcal{K}$-valued analytic functions on $\mathbb{D}$.
