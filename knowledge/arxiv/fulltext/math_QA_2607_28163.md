## Abstract

Double shuffle Lie algebra $\mathfrak{dmr}_0$ was introduced by G.~Racinet in the algebraic study of the multiple zeta values. In this note, we prove that for any $ψ\in \mathfrak{dmr}_0$, it satisfies the infinitesimal hexagon equation $[ψ(x,y),x]+[ψ(-x-y,y),-x-y]=0$. The proof is through the comparison of two different Hopf algebras.

## 1.Introduction

## 1.1.Multiple zeta values (MZVs) and double shuffle Lie algebra

Multiple zeta values are real numbersζ​(s1,…,sl)=∑n1>n2>…>nl≥11n1s1​n2s2​…​nlsl,\zeta(s_{1},\ldots,s_{l})=\sum_{n_{1}>n_{2}>\ldots>n_{l}\geq 1}\frac{1}{n^{s_{1}}_{1}n^{s_{2}}_{2}\ldots n^{s_{l}}_{l}},

withsi≥1s_{i}\geq 1for1≤i≤l1\leq i\leq lands1≥2s_{1}\geq 2. Those numbers generalize the Riemann zeta function at integer values. In the case ofl=2l=2, they were already studied by Euler back to the 17th century. MZVs were rediscovered independently by D. Zagier and J. Ecalle. MZVs have three distinguished set of relations, see for example[11], namely the stuffle relation, shuffle relation and regularization relations, those relations has deep connection with the theory of motives[4,5,2]. Later in[14], G. Racinet introduced a Lie algebra𝔡​𝔪​𝔯0\mathfrak{dmr}_{0}and use it to study formally those three set of relations.

## 1.2.Grothendieck-Teichmuller Lie algebra and infinitesimal Hexagon equation

The Grothendieck-Teichmuller Lie algebra(𝔤​𝔯​𝔱1)(\mathfrak{grt}_{1})plays a central role in many different branch of mathematics. The infinitesimal hexagon equation was introduced initially by Drinfeld in the definitions of the Grothendieck-Teichmuller Lie algebra𝔤​𝔯​𝔱1\mathfrak{grt}_{1}[6]and Ihara in the symmetric outer derivation Lie algebra[12]. Later it was proved by Furusho[9]that the pentagon equation implies the infinitesimal hexagon equation and the relation that𝔤​𝔯​𝔱1↪𝔡​𝔪​𝔯0\mathfrak{grt}_{1}\hookrightarrow\mathfrak{dmr}_{0}in[10].

## 1.3.Kashiwara-Vergne Lie algebra and special derivation property

The Kashiwara-Vergne Lie algebra𝔨​𝔯​𝔳2\mathfrak{krv}_{2}is introduced in[1]in their study of Kashiwara-Vergne problem[1], they introduce the tangential derivation Lie algebra𝔱​𝔡​𝔢​𝔯2\mathfrak{tder}_{2}which are derivations of the free Lie algebra of two generators𝕃​(x,y)\mathbb{L}(x,y)that sendsx↦[a,x]x\mapsto[a,x]andy↦[b,y]y\mapsto[b,y], the special derivations are the ones that satisfies[a,x]+[b,y]=0[a,x]+[b,y]=0. Up to inner derivations, we could formulate special derivations property as[a,x]+[b,z]=0[a,x]+[b,z]=0wherez=−x−yz=-x-y. In the recent papers[15,13,8], the following conjecture of Ecalle[7]is proved. We use the form in[8],

## 1.4.Main results

## Theorem 1.2.

For everyψ∈𝔡​𝔪​𝔯0\psi\in\mathfrak{dmr}_{0}, it satisfies the following infinitesimal hexagon equation(2)[ψ​(x,y),x]+[ψ​(−x−y,y),−x−y]=0.[\psi(x,y),x]+[\psi(-x-y,y),-x-y]=0.

## Corollary 1.3.

Forψ∈𝔡​𝔪​𝔯0\psi\in\mathfrak{dmr}_{0}, we have[ψ​(x,−x−y),x]+[ψ​(y,−x−y),y]=0,[ψ​(y,x),y]+[ψ​(−x−y,x),−x−y]=0[\psi(x,-x-y),x]+[\psi(y,-x-y),y]=0,\quad[\psi(y,x),y]+[\psi(-x-y,x),-x-y]=0
