## Abstract

We introduce Schur-Agler type class for the symmetrized polydisc $\mathbb{G}_d$ and establish a realization theorem for functions in this class. We state and prove an interpolation theorem on $\mathbb{G}_d$ with interpolating functions belonging to the associated Schur-Agler type class. Moreover, Toeplitz corona and extension theorems are established for $\mathbb{G}_d$. We also extend the realization, interpolation, Toeplitz corona and extension theorems to a more general symmetrized family of domains $Θ_d$.

## 1.Introduction

This kernel dependent formulation provides a natural framework for studying interpolation problems on more general domains inℂd\mathbb{C}^{d}. For example, Abrahamse[3]presented a similar interpolation theorem on anmm-holed planar domainRRin terms of a family of Pick matrices. A more general approach to interpolation via families of kernels can be found in[39]. A criterion for Nevanlinna-Pick interpolation problem on the bidisc𝔻2\mathbb{D}^{2}was presented in[8,9]via a certain family of kernels on𝔻2\mathbb{D}^{2}. These interpolation results naturally motivate the following interpolation problem. Supposez1,…,znz_{1},\dotsc,z_{n}are distinct points in a domainΩ⊆ℂd\Omega\subseteq\mathbb{C}^{d}andλ1,…,λn∈𝔻¯\lambda_{1},\dotsc,\lambda_{n}\in\overline{\mathbb{D}}. The solution to this problem depends crucially on the geometry of the underlying domainΩ\Omega. A natural approach to the Nevanlinna-Pick interpolation problem on a domain is to first identify an appropriate class of interpolating functions together with an intrinsic characterization of that class. Such a characterization is commonly referred to as arealization theorem. Two classes of functions that arise naturally in this context are the Schur class and the Schur-Agler class. Several domains arising as images of classical domains under proper holomorphic mappings have attracted considerable attention in multivariable operator theory and function theory. Among them, the symmetrized bidisc[11], the tetrablock[2]and the pentablock[6]play a prominent role. The symmetrized bidisc𝔾2\mathbb{G}_{2}is the image of the bidisc𝔻2\mathbb{D}^{2}under the symmetrization map(z1,z2)↦(z1+z2,z1​z2),(z_{1},z_{2})\mapsto(z_{1}+z_{2},z_{1}z_{2}),

The tetrablock𝔼\mathbb{E}and the pentablockℙ\mathbb{P}are further examples of domains whose function theory exhibits connections with operator theory and matrix-valued interpolation. The realization and interpolation theorems for the symmetrized bidisc𝔾2\mathbb{G}_{2}were obtained in[15,26]with different methods. For the tetrablock𝔼\mathbb{E}, the realization and interpolation theorems were obtained by the authors of[38]. For the pentablockℙ\mathbb{P}, the realization and interpolation theorems were presented in[48]. An application of realization and interpolation theorems includes the extension problems for domains. This question is referred to as the norm-preserving extension problem onΩ\Omega. This problem has been explored for several important domains inℂd\mathbb{C}^{d}, namely the bidisc[10], the symmetrized bidisc[26], the tetrablock[38]and the pentablock[48].

Realization theorems for function classes on domains inℂd\mathbb{C}^{d}also provide an important framework to study the Toeplitz corona problem.
