## Abstract

We prove the generalized total rank conjecture over regular rings in characteristic $2$: if $R$ is   a regular Noetherian   domain of characteristic $2$ and $P$ is a differential $R$-module admitting a finite projective   flag and having nonzero homology $H(P)$, then $\rank_R(P)\ge2^{\codim_RH(P)}$. In particular, we   prove Carlsson's conjecture for elementary abelian $2$-groups in every rank. We also   obtain sharp homology bounds for arbitrary continuous actions of such groups and for perfect complexes   over finite group algebras; the sphere rank conjecture follows. The proof identifies the homology   of a chain model for the $C_2$-Tate construction on $P\otimes_RP$ with the Frobenius pullback of   $H(P)$, and compares lengths by deforming the Tate   differential.

## 1.Introduction

Conjectures about total ranks of free complexes and about the homology of spaces with free group
actions lie at the intersection of commutative algebra and algebraic topology. On the topological
side, Carlsson[GCar83]conjectured[GCar86, Conjecture I.3]that a
nonempty finite free(ℤ/2)d(\mathbb{Z}/2)^{d}-CW complexXXsatisfies∑idim𝔽2Hi​(X;𝔽2)≥2d\sum_{i}\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{2}}H_{i}(X;\mathbb{F}_{2})\geq 2^{d}, and proved it ford≤3d\leq 3[GCar87, Theorem 2]. On the algebraic side, the Buchsbaum–Eisenbud–Horrocks
conjecture[BE77,Har79]predicts that a nonzero finite-length module of finite
projective dimension over a Noetherian local ring of dimensionddhasiith Betti number at least(di)\binom{d}{i}. Summing these inequalities gives the total rank conjecture, proposed by Avramov and
formulated and studied by Avramov–Buchweitz[AB93]: the total Betti number is at
least2d2^{d}. The corresponding

finite-length homology over a local ringRRof dimensionddsatisfiesrankR⁡(P):=∑irankR⁡Pi≥2d\operatorname{rank}_{R}(P):=\sum_{i}\operatorname{rank}_{R}P_{i}\geq 2^{d}.

This extension to complexes, known as thegeneralized total rank conjecture, is false in odd
characteristic: Iyengar and Walker[IW18]produced counterexamples, even over regular
local rings, and refuted the algebraic analogue of Carlsson’s conjecture for(ℤ/p)d(\mathbb{Z}/p)^{d}withppodd; these complexes are not topologically realizable[RS22]. In contrast,
Walker[Wal17]proved the total rank conjecture for modules in odd characteristic. Neither
the counterexamples nor Walker’s theorem addressed characteristic22. Evidence that characteristic22is exceptional came from[VW25], which proved a statement about sufficiently short complexes
known to be false in every odd characteristic. Our main theorem proves the generalized conjecture
over regular rings of characteristic22, and Carlsson’s conjecture in full.

## Remark 3.6.
