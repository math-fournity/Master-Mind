## Abstract

We describe the image of a quantized BFN Coulomb branch𝒜G,Nℏ=1\mathcal{A}_{G,N}^{\hbar=1}under localization (abelianization) map for anyG,NG,N. In most cases, such as quiver gauge theories without loops, this description works for any flavors, sometimes we need to take generic or formal flavor parameters. The answer is given by a roots and residue condition similar to Ginzburg—Kapranov—Vasserot construction of DAHA[GKV]. As a corollary, for any quantized conical Coulomb branch (and zero or small flavors) we provide a mathematical construction of the sphere trace introduced by Gaiotto and Okazaki[GO].
