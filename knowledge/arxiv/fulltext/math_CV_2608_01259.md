## Abstract

Motivated by the gap phenomenon for proper holomorphic maps between complex unit balls, this paper investigates smooth CR maps $f$ from an open piece $M$ of the Shilov boundary of the unit ball $B^s$ into the Shilov boundary $S_{r',s'}$ of a higher-rank Type I bounded symmetric domain $Ω_{r',s'}$. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first work to systematically establish a general gap phenomenon in the higher-rank setting. Our main result demonstrates that when the signature difference $s'-r'$ falls into the interval $k(s-1) \le s'-r' < (k+1)(s-1)$ for some integer $k$, the non-trivial component of $f$ is constrained to a much smaller Type I boundary. Up to automorphisms of the domain and target spaces, $f$ decomposes into the block-diagonal form $$f(z) = \begin{pmatrix} I_{r'-k} & 0 \\ 0 & φ(z) \end{pmatrix},$$ where the essential component $φ: M \to S_{k, s'-r'+k}$ is a smooth CR map into the corresponding Shilov boundary. We provide explicit constructions showing that these dimensional bounds are sharp. As an immediate corollary, in the initial gap regime $s-1 \le s' - r' < 2s-2$, the map $f$ reduces to the standard linear embedding.

## 1Introduction

The rigidity of holomorphic and Cauchy-Riemann (CR) maps between bounded symmetric domains is a classical topic in several complex variables. While the rank 1 case (complex unit balls) is deeply understood from Poincaré to the seminal works of Cima-Suffridge[CS],D’Angelo[Da1,Da2], Faran[Fa], Forstnerič[F1,F2], Huang[Hu1,Hu2], Huang-Ji[HJ]and others, rigidity phenomena in higher-rank and mixed-rank settings remain largely open.

A celebrated feature of the rank11setting is thegap phenomenon. For instance, Faran[Fa]proved that for target dimensionsN∈[n+1,2​n−2]N\in[n+1,2n-2], any rational proper map fromBn{B}^{n}toBN{B}^{N}is equivalent to the standard linear embedding. Subsequent works by Huang-Ji-Xu[HJX]and Huang-Ji-Yin[HJY]discovered that similar gap phenomena recur over higher intervals, culminating in the general Gap Conjecture[HJY2].

In contrast to the rank11setting, mappings into higher-rank bounded symmetric domains pose significant challenges. When both domains have rank at least22(i.e.,2≤rank​(Ω)≤rank​(Ω′)2\leq\text{rank}(\Omega)\leq\text{rank}(\Omega^{\prime})), substantial structural rigidity has been established. For comprehensive results in this direction, we refer the reader to the works of Chan[Ch1,Ch2], Henkin-Novikov[HN], Kim[Kim1], Kim-Mok-Seo[KMS], Kim-Zaitsev[KZ13,KZ15], Mok[Mok], Mok-Tsai[MT], Mok-Ng-Tu[MNT], Ng[Ng1,Ng2,Ng3], Seo[Seo1,Seo2,Seo3], and Tu[Tu1,Tu2], to name a few.

From a broader geometric perspective, it is natural to expect that systematic gap phenomena are not exclusive to rank11domains, but should universally exist for mappings between bounded symmetric domains of arbitrary ranks. As a first step toward uncovering such a universal gap theory, it is strategically crucial to understand the transition from rank11to higher-rank spaces. However, this mixed-rank case, specifically, mappings from rank11complex balls into higher-rank Type I domains resist classical approaches. Traditional tools like the Tanaka-Chern-Moser theory[CM,Ta]and the Cartan moving frame method do not easily adapt to this mixed-rank setting.

Recently, important progress has been made by Kim and Zaitsev[KZ13], and Kim[Kim2]in studying the rigidities of local CR mappings between Shilov boundaries of Type I bounded symmetric domains. Notably, Kim[Kim2]studied nonconstant smooth CR maps from the sphereS1,sS_{1,s}toSr′,s′S_{r^{\prime},s^{\prime}}, revealing a specific initial gap interval[2​s−2,3​s−4][2s-2,3s-4]. Despite this breakthrough, whether a systematic, multi-interval gap phenomenon analogous to the celebrated Gap Conjecture in the rank11setting exists for higher-rank bounded symmetric domains has remained unknown. In this paper, we provide the first systematic investigation of this problem and establish the general, sharp gap intervals for CR maps from rank11Type I Shilov boundaries to higher-rank Type I Shilov boundaries.
