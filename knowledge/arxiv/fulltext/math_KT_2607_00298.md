Formulas such as the ones we are developing in this paper are topological markers in physical models such as in[12],[14], and[15]. These methods have been used to study topological insulators and more recently these methods have been proposed to classify topology in photonic systems as in[9],[10],[8],[11].
We wish to be able to address physical models in a variety of possible dimensions, with a variety of symmetry structures, which may or may not contain time-reversal symmetry, and which may or may not contain particle-hole symmetry. Together with the previous paper[5], this article extends the range of such models that can be addressed to include all spheres with involution. The formulas that we develop in this paper should be seen asK\displaystyle K-theory generalizations of the index formulas described in[13], particularly Section 5.
