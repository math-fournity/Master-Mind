## Abstract

Gill (arXiv:2402.13376) introduced the probabilistic automatic complexity $A_P(w)$ of a finite string $w$: the least number of states of a probabilistic finite automaton (PFA) for which $w$ is the unique most probably accepted string of its length. He asked whether $A_P$ is unbounded, noting that no string with $A_P > 3$ was known (Question 4.14 of that paper). We answer the question by proving that $A_P(w)\le 3$ for every string $w$ over every finite alphabet. The witnessing three-state automaton is explicit: its reduced dynamics tracks the pair $(u,u^2)$, where $u$ is the reversed base-$b$ value of the input, and its acceptance functional is a downward parabola peaked at the value of the target string. Combined with Gill's classification of the binary strings with $A_P=2$, this completely determines $A_P$ on binary strings.

## Corollary 5.1.

## Remark 5.3.

The computability ofAPA_{P}was open in[1], where it was
shown that the relationAP​(w)≤kA_{P}(w)\leq kis computably enumerable but
neither the co-enumerability nor the computability ofAPA_{P}itself was
established. Corollary5.2settles this for binary
strings by brute force: the function is eventually a two-valued,
regularly-described object. For alphabets of sizeb≥3b\geq 3,
Theorem1.2still pinsAPA_{P}to{2,3}\{2,3\}, and
computability would follow from a classification of the strings withAP=2A_{P}=2over such alphabets, which[1]does not carry out.

## Remark 5.4.

Theorem1.2also bears on the other questions of[1]. Question 4.16 (a notion ofAPA_{P}-randomness) becomes
vacuous: no string attains a length-dependent maximum, which in
hindsight explains the empirical wall at33observed in[1]. The measure is coarse for a structural reason made
explicit by the construction: the real-valued transition probabilities
of a three-state PFA can absorb the entire description ofww(here,
in the single parameters=2​v​(w)s=2\,v(w)). By contrast, the gap achieved by
our witness decays likeb−2​nb^{-2n}, so the refined measures proposed in[1, §§5–6]—the parameterized complexityAP,δA_{P,\delta}for fixedδ>0\delta>0, the gap structure functionγk​(w)=maxA⁡gapA⁡(w)\gamma^{k}(w)=\max_{A}\operatorname{gap}_{A}(w), and the measure-weighted variantsAμA_{\mu}—remain genuinely graded and are, we would argue, the right

automata, Theorem1.2givesA𝒢​(w)≤3A_{\mathcal{G}}(w)\leq 3in the
