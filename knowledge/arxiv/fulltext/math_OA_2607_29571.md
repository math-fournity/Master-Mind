## Abstract

We prove the existence of strongly converging unitary representations in various new settings of countable groups, in particular, arising as extensions by exact groups: semidirect products with amenable groups; generalized wreath products with abelian base; graph wreath products; various free-by-cyclic groups including Gersten's group; and general Bernoulli shift crossed products on $C^*$-algebras.

## Abstract.

We prove the existence of strongly converging unitary representations in various new settings of countable groups, in particular, arising as extensions by exact groups: semidirect products with amenable groups; generalized wreath products with abelian base; graph wreath products; various free-by-cyclic groups including Gersten’s group; and general Bernoulli shift crossed products onC∗C^{*}-algebras.

## 1.Introduction

In the work[10], Haagerup and Thorbjornsen strengthened Voiculescu’s asymptotic freeness theorem[24]into the regime ofstrong convergence. In particular, their result implies that the nonabelian free group𝔽2=⟨a,b⟩\mathbb{F}_{2}=\langle a,b\rangleadmits a sequence of unitary representationsρn:G→𝒰​(𝕄n​(ℂ))\rho_{n}:G\to\mathcal{U}(\mathbb{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C}))such that for any noncommutative∗*-polynomialPPon two variables,‖ρn​(P​(a,b))‖→‖P​(a,b)‖\|\rho_{n}(P(a,b))\|\to\|P(a,b)\|where the norm on the right hand side is in the left regular representation. Despite originally havingC∗C^{*}-algebraic motivations[25], strong convergence as a program of research has multiple interdisciplinary applications, including but not limited to von Neumann algebras[11], spectral graph theory[3], optimal spectral gaps[12], minimal surface theory[22]. We refer the reader to the recent surveys[23,16]for more references and applications.

Concerning strong convergence properties for countable groups, we isolate three existential notions that we concern ourselves with in this paper:
- (1)

Recent years have witnessed a substantial study into these properties[3,13,15,14,16,20,19,4]. In a recent development[5], a newC∗C^{*}-algebraic approach was introduced and used to prove these properties in vast new families of countable groups. Most notably,[5]proved the PFF property in broad generality: virtually special groups such as fundamental groups of closed hyperbolic 3-manifolds, free products, group doubles, graph products, and more general amalgamated free products.

In the present article we expand the family of countable groups admitting strongly converging unitary representations in a different direction. We focus here onextensionsby exact groups. Before we state our results, we first remark that exactness is a natural assumption. Indeed, even in the case of trivial actions, i.e, direct products, preservation of strong convergence is unknown in the absence of this assumption. Now we describe some insights concerning the proofs of our results. The recent works[7,5], emphasized a general strategy of proving the existence of strongly converging unitary representations involving two stages. The first stage is to set upambient strong convergence. The second stage involves anupgrading procedureto obtain the full strong convergence of matrix models. Ambient strong convergence is typically set up for one of the building blocks of the groups in question using existing strongly convergent models. The precise role of ambient strong convergence is to be able to locate the entire reducedC∗C^{*}-algebra of the building block (not just the group ring) inside a large norm–ultraproductC∗C^{*}-algebra via a sequence of desirable maps. This then allows one to useC∗C^{*}-algebraic techniques to design upgrading procedures, to extend the embedding to the entire reducedC∗C^{*}-algebra of the group in question. In[5], the upgrading procedure was carried out using the remarkable work of Pimsner[17]. More generally, in[7], a general machine called “Toeplitz exactness” was proved, to upgrade strong convergence into Toeplitz–PimnserC∗C^{*}-algebras associated to arbitraryC∗C^{*}-correspondences via the gauge–invariant uniqueness theorem. In the present paper, the upgrading is achieved via a rather direct and succinct argument inspired by a key idea in[19], circumventing the above deep machinery. We are able to carry out a rather elementary “double crossed product” trick (Proposition2.3) combined with Fell’s absorption and exactness to carry out upgrading for extensions. Proving the necessary ambient strong convergence is the rest of the puzzle, wherein we have to design such models in each of the cases addressed in our results, often using the specific algebraic and combinatorial information.

We take this opportunity to formulate two natural open questions. Firstly, it is natural to now ask if all free-by-cyclic groups are PFF. More generally, what about free-by-free groups? A specific instance of this is whether the pure braid group on four strands is PFF.
