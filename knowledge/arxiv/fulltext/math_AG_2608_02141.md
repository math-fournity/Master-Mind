## Abstract

For quotient stacks [X/G] with X a smooth quasi-projective geometrically integral k-variety and G a linear k-group, where k is a number field, we extend some relations between local-global obstructions on varieties, showing that étale-Brauer obstruction is the finest one among various obstructions (such as iterated descent, finite descent, descent, Brauer-Manin}).
