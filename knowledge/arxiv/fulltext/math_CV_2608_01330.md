## Abstract

Let $(X,ω)$ be a compact Kähler manifold and let $V\subset X$ be a closed complex submanifold. Coman-Guedj-Zeriahi proposed the problem: is every $ω|_V$-plurisubharmonic function on $V$ the restriction of an $ω$-plurisubharmonic function on $X$? In this paper, we solve this problem affirmatively, even for a compact Hermitian manifold.

## 1.Introduction

Let(X,ω)(X,\omega)be a compact Kähler manifold andV⊂XV\subset Xa closed complex submanifold. Throughout this paper, we assume thatV≠XV\neq X. A functionu:X→[−∞,+∞)u\colon X\to[-\infty,+\infty)is called quasi-plurisubharmonic (qpsh for short) if locally it can be written as the sum of a plurisubharmonic function and a smooth function. We fix the following notations:dc:=i2​π​(∂¯−∂)d^{c}:=\frac{i}{2\pi}(\bar{\partial}-\partial)andωV:=ω|V\omega_{V}:=\omega|_{V}.
DefineQPSH⁡(X):={u:u​is qpsh on​X}\operatorname{QPSH}(X):=\{u:u\ \text{is qpsh on}\ X\},PSH⁡(X,ω):={u∈QPSH⁡(X):ω+d​dc​u≥0}\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega):=\{u\in\operatorname{QPSH}(X):\omega+dd^{c}u\geq 0\}andPSH⁡(X,ω,δ):={u∈QPSH⁡(X):ω+d​dc​u≥δ​ω}\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega,\delta):=\{u\in\operatorname{QPSH}(X):\omega+dd^{c}u\geq\delta\omega\}.

In this paper, we study the following problem raised by Coman-Guedj-Zeriahi.

## Problem 1.1([CGZ13, p. 40],[DGZ16, Problem 37]).

Does the following holdPSH​(V,ω|V)=PSH​(X,ω)|V​?\displaystyle\mbox{PSH}(V,\omega|_{V})=\mbox{PSH}(X,\omega)|_{V}?

A related and strict problem asks whether every strictlyωV\omega_{V}-plurisubharmonic potential onVVis the restriction of a strictlyω\omega-plurisubharmonic potential onXX, or equivalently, whether every Kähler current in[ωV][\omega_{V}]extends to a Kähler current in[ω][\omega]. Here[ωV][\omega_{V}](resp.[ω][\omega]) is the Bott-Chern cohomology class ofωV\omega_{V}(resp.ω\omega) inH1,1​(V,ℝ)H^{1,1}(V,\mathbb{R})(resp.H1,1​(X,ℝ)H^{1,1}(X,\mathbb{R})).

Several cases were known. The Hodge case was obtained in[CGZ13]; the real Néron–Severi case, including singular analytic subvarieties, was treated in[CGZ22]. Smooth strictly plurisubharmonic functions extend by[CGZ13, Proposition 2.1], see also[Sch98]. Collins-Tosatti proved the strict extension theorem for currents with analytic singularities[CT14](see also[DRWXZ26]for a refinement). Ning-Wang-Zhou proved the arbitrary-singularity strict theorem under the existence of a holomorphic retraction from a neighborhood ofVVontoVV[NWZ24], see also the earlier tubular-neighborhood result by Wang-Zhou[WZ20].

In this paper, we give a positive answer to the1.1in full generality.

## Theorem 1.1.

Let(X,ω)(X,\omega)be a compact Hermitian manifold and letV⊂XV\subset Xbe a closed complex submanifold. Then everyφ∈PSH⁡(V,ωV)\varphi\in\operatorname{PSH}(V,\omega_{V})admits an extensionΦ∈PSH⁡(X,ω)\Phi\in\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)such thatΦ|V=φ\left.\Phi\right|_{V}=\varphi.
Equivalently,PSH⁡(X,ω)|V=PSH⁡(V,ωV)\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega)|_{V}=\operatorname{PSH}(V,\omega_{V}).

As a direct application, we get the following theorem.

## Theorem 1.2.

Let(X,ω)(X,\omega)andVVbe as in1.1. Suppose thatφ∈PSH⁡(V,ωV,ε)\varphi\in\operatorname{PSH}(V,\omega_{V},\varepsilon)for someε>0\varepsilon>0. Then there areε′>0\varepsilon^{\prime}>0andΦ∈PSH⁡(X,ω,ε′)\Phi\in\operatorname{PSH}(X,\omega,\varepsilon^{\prime})such thatΦ|V=φ\left.\Phi\right|_{V}=\varphi.
Consequently, ifω\omegais a Kähler metric, then every Kähler current in[ωV][\omega_{V}]is the restriction of a Kähler current in[ω][\omega].

## Remark 1.1.

1.1and1.2do not require(X,ω)(X,\omega)to be compact Kähler. To prove1.1, we follow the strategy of[NWZ24]while introducing a new technique. The main novelty over previous results[NWZ24]is that we do not insist on simultaneously extending the smooth approximationsφj\varphi_{j}ofφ\varphionVVto a common neighborhood ofVVwith uniform estimate, and instead, we relax the uniformity of extending data while preserving monotonicity. The argument could apply to the non-smooth case, i.e.,VVa subvariety ofXX. This will be studied in a subsequent paper.
