## Abstract

This is an expository paper based on the authors' joint works. The goal is to explain the statements and the ideas behind two conjectures on associated varieties and simple modules of simple affine vertex algebras $L_k(\mathfrak{g})$ for a simple and simply-laced Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ and a integer level $k$ above the critical level.

## 3.Motivation from 4D mirror symmetry

We discuss briefly some recent developments coming from mathematical physics that also motivated our work.

Although vertex operator algebras (VOAs) are known to provide a rigorous mathematical framework for two dimensional chiral conformal field theories, a recent breakthrough discovery by Beem-Lemos-Liendo-Peelaers-Rastelli-van Rees[BLLPR+15]showed that they also appear as almost complete invariants of four dimensional𝒩=2\mathcal{N}=2super conformal field theories (SCFT). More precisely, they construct a map𝕍\mathbb{V}sending a 4D𝒩=2\mathcal{N}=2SCFT𝒯\mathcal{T}to a VOA𝕍​(𝒯)\mathbb{V}(\mathcal{T}). Conjecturally this map is finite-to-one, and the Higgs branch of𝒯\mathcal{T}is expected to coincide with the associated varietyX𝕍​(𝒯)X_{\mathbb{V}(\mathcal{T})}of𝕍​(𝒯)\mathbb{V}(\mathcal{T})[BR18]. Since the simple affine vertex algebrasLk​(𝔤)L_{k}(\mathfrak{g})sometimes appear as𝕍​(𝒯)\mathbb{V}(\mathcal{T})or as extensions of𝕍​(𝒯)\mathbb{V}(\mathcal{T})’s, it is essential from this perspective to determine whichLk​(𝔤)L_{k}(\mathfrak{g})is quasi-lisse.

Mirror symmetries also come into this picture. For 3D supersymmetric gauge theories, mirror symmetry involves two hyperkähler manifoldsXXandYY. By the work of Braden-Licata-Proudfoot-Webster[BLPW10,BLPW16,BLPW16a], the relation betweenXXandYYcan be partly understood as a duality between geometric information extracted fromYYand representation theoretic data from the category𝒪\mathcal{O}of a quantization𝒜X\mathcal{A}_{X}ofXX(and its resolution). Many aspects of this proposal were confirmed in the subsequent work[SXY24]of Shan-Xie-Yan in the case of boundary admissible levels.
