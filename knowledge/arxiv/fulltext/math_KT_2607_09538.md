## Abstract.

We algebraically construct exterior power operations on higherrelativealgebraicKK-groups and prove their desired properties such as the expected behaviour with respect to (tensor) products and composition. This builds on Grayson’s description of relativeKK-groups in terms of explicit generators and relations and on work by Harris, the first author and Taelman for (absolute)KK-groups. Among the new features in our approach is the observation that the product axiom in the classical notion of aλ\lambda-ring is redundant.

## Introduction

Arguably most powerful and fundamental among the features of higher algebraicKK-theory are long exact sequences ofKK-groups. The most general type of these is the long exact sequence…→Kn+1​ℳ→Kn+1​𝒩→Kn​[F]→Kn​ℳ→Kn​𝒩→…\ldots\rightarrow K_{n+1}{\mathcal{M}}\rightarrow K_{n+1}{\mathcal{N}}\rightarrow K_{n}[F]\rightarrow K_{n}{\mathcal{M}}\rightarrow K_{n}{\mathcal{N}}\rightarrow\ldots(1)

Building on his earlier work in[Gra12]for (absolute)KK-groups using so-called binary complexes, Grayson has in[Gra16]given a conjectural description ofKn​[F]K_{n}[F]in terms of generators and relations and established the sequence (1) purely algebraically. This conjectural description ofKn​[F]K_{n}[F]is confirmed in[Tu25]to be equivalent to the definition given above, and we use it as the definition ofKn​[F]K_{n}[F]in this paper.

Exterior power operations onKK-groups provide another important tool in the (higher)KK-theory of schemes.
They are the core ingredients in the theory of Adams operations and, more generally, in Grothendieck’s seminal Riemann-Roch theory. Their properties, such as their behaviour with respect to (tensor) products and composition, are captured in the classical, abstract notion of aλ\lambda-ring.

The object of this paper is to prove the following theorem, seeDefinition5.3andTheorem5.4, which combines the two features mentioned above.
