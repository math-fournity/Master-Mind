## Abstract

Whether the Scott space of the complete Boolean algebraRO⁡(ℝ)\operatorname{RO}(\mathbb{R})of regular open subsets in the real line is sober was explicitly
recorded as an open problem by Xu and Zhao and was reiterated in a later
survey. We give a negative answer to this problem in this paper. The proof has two ingredients.
First, forB=RO⁡(ℝ)B=\operatorname{RO}(\mathbb{R}), we
show thatΔB={(p,q)∈B×B:p∧q=0},\Delta_{B}=\{(p,q)\in B\times B:p\wedge q=0\},

is an irreducible Scott-closed subset of the product posetB×BB\times B, which is not the closure of a point. Finally,RO⁡(ℝ)≅RO⁡(ℝ)×RO⁡(ℝ)\operatorname{RO}(\mathbb{R})\cong\operatorname{RO}(\mathbb{R})\times\operatorname{RO}(\mathbb{R})is not a sober space under the Scott topology.

Keywords.Scott topology; sober space; complete Boolean algebra; regular open set; irreducible closed set.

2020 Mathematics Subject Classification.Primary 06B35; Secondary 54A05, 54D99.

## 1Introduction

For a posetPP, the Scott topology is the topology whose open sets are
the upper sets inaccessible by existing directed suprema. Scott spaces
are central in domain theory, where sobriety permits the recovery of
points from irreducible closed sets; see Scott’s foundational paper[SCO72]and the standard references[GHK+03,GOU13]. Continuous dcpos have sober
Scott spaces, but Scott sobriety fails in general. Johnstone constructed
the first nonsober dcpo[JOH81], and Isbell subsequently
obtained a nonsober complete lattice[ISB82]. Later work
produced, among other examples, a complete Heyting algebra with nonsober
Scott space[XXZ21]and a countable complete lattice with
nonsober Scott space[MXL+22].

A particularly natural unresolved test case has been the complete Boolean
algebraB=RO⁡(ℝ)B=\operatorname{RO}(\mathbb{R})

of regular open subsets of the real line, ordered by inclusion. Erne and
Gatzke showed that binary joins on this algebra are not jointly Scott
continuous[EG85]. The sobriety question was subsequently
attributed to Erne and explicitly formulated by Xu and Zhao[XZ21, Question 3.6]; see also[XU23, Question 3.6]. The more general question whether
there exists any complete Boolean algebra with a nonsober Scott space was
recorded alongside it[XZ21,XU23, Question 3.7].

The main result of this paper gives negative answers to both questions.

## Theorem 1.1(Main theorem).

The Scott spaces ofRO⁡(ℝ)\operatorname{RO}(\mathbb{R})is not sober.

## 2.2Complete Boolean algebras

We write0,1,∧,∨0,1,\wedge,\vee, and¬\negfor the operations of a complete
Boolean algebraBB, and putB+=B∖{0}B^{+}=B\setminus\{0\}. We repeatedly use
the infinitary distributive identitiesb∧⋁A\displaystyle b\wedge\bigvee A=⋁a∈A(b∧a),\displaystyle=\bigvee_{a\in A}(b\wedge a),(2)b∨⋀A\displaystyle b\vee\bigwedge A=⋀a∈A(b∨a).\displaystyle=\bigwedge_{a\in A}(b\vee a).(3)

Indeed,b∧(−)b\wedge(-)is left adjoint to¬b∨(−)\neg b\vee(-)and therefore
preserves arbitrary joins; the second identity is the order dual of the
first. In particular, maps obtained by taking the meet with a fixed
element preserve arbitrary joins and hence are Scott continuous.

Boolean complementation reverses the order and interchanges the Scott
and dual-Scott topologies.

For finite families, the meet is ordinary intersection. Notice that a
Boolean join need not be the ordinary union. Further background on
regular open algebras may be found in[BD07].
