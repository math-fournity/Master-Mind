## Abstract.

Our earlier results on effective codescent morphisms of Hausdorff topological spaces are strengthened and complemented. In particular, it is proved that any embeddingp:B↣Ep:B\rightarrowtail Ewith compactBBand normalEEis an effective codescent morphism in the category of Hausdorff topological spaces.

Key words and phrases: effective codescent morphism; codescent morphism; Hausdorff topological space.

2020 Mathematics Subject Classification: 18C20, 18F60, 54B30, 54C05, 54B99.

## 1.Introduction

Let𝒞\mathcal{C}be a category with pushouts, andp:B→Ep:B\rightarrow Ebe a morphism in𝒞\mathcal{C}. Recall thatppinduces the following adjunction between the coslice categories: the left adjoint is the change-of-cobase functorp∗:E/𝒞→B/𝒞p^{*}:E/\mathcal{C}\rightarrow B/\mathcal{C}

(pushing out alongpp), while the right adjoint is the functorp!:B/𝒞→E/𝒞p^{!}:B/\mathcal{C}\rightarrow E/\mathcal{C}

that composes withppon the right. The morphismppis called a codescent morphism (resp. an effective codescent morphism) if the functorp∗p^{*}is precomonadic (resp. comonadic)[2]. If a category has equalizers, then a morphism is a codescent morphism if and only if it is a universal regular monomorphism, i.e., a regular monomorphism such that its pushout along any morphism is again a regular monomorphism[1].

In[6], we proved that every codescent morphism is effective in the categoryH​a​u​sHausof Hausdorff topological spaces. However, not every regular monomorphism (= closed embedding) is a codescent morphism in this category, as it follows from the example provided in[3, Remark 5.15]. Therefore, the problem of characterizing codescent morphisms in the categoryH​a​u​sHausarose. In[6], we gave a necessary condition for a regular monomorphismB↣EB\rightarrowtail Eof Hausdorff topological spaces to be universal. Moreover, it was proved that ifBBis compact andEEis regular, then this condition is also sufficient.

In the present paper, we strengthen and complement the original results. In particular, the requirement thatEEis regular is removed from the second assertion. Moreover, we give a new sufficient condition for a closed embeddingB↣EB\rightarrowtail Eof Hausdorff topological spaces to be a universal regular monomorphism. Besides, we prove that ifBBis compact, then this condition is also necessary. With the aid of these facts, it is shown that ifBBis compact andEEis normal, thenppis an effective codescent morphism in the category of Hausdorff topological spaces. In particular, this is the case ifEEis compact and Hausdorff.
