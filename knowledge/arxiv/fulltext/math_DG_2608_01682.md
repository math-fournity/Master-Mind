## 1.Introduction

## 1.1.Singular asymptotically hyperbolic manifolds

The positive mass theorem (PMT) is a foundational result in Riemannian geometry and general relativity, characterizing the nonnegativity of the total mass of manifolds with nonnegative scalar curvature and standard asymptotic fall-off behavior.

In recent years, extensive progress has been made on the nonexistence of metrics with positive scalar curvature (PSC) on singular spaces and the corresponding positive mass theorems. In[LM19,Kaz24,WX24], the authors established Geroch-type conjectures for certain smooth, uniformly Euclidean metrics that are smooth outside a codimension-33submanifold. In[DSW24], the authors considered related problems for manifolds with isolated conical singularities.[DWWW24]established global rigidity in general settings using methods from the RCD theory.[JSZ22]and[CLZ22]established positive mass theorems forC0∩W1,pC^{0}\cap W^{1,p}metrics. More recently,[HSY26](see also[HSY25]) and[BHH+26]have dealt with PMT for higher-dimensional asymptotically flat (AF) manifolds with a singular set. In[BQ08], the authors obtained PMT for spin asymptotically hyperbolic (AH) manifolds with corners along compact hypersurfaces. Most recently,[KWW26]proves the positive mass theorem for AF manifolds with anL∞L^{\infty}-metric when the codimension is slightly larger than 3, and[BZ26]proves the Geroch-type conjectures in similar settings.

The primary motivation of this paper is to investigate which classes of singularities yield positive contributions to the PMT in the asymptotically hyperbolic setting. Obviously, the singularity arising in negative-mass AdS-Schwarzschild manifolds exhibits negative mass contributions. We note that singularities satisfying RCD conditions are those near which both the Ricci curvature and, consequently, the scalar curvature are bounded below in a suitable sense. Intuitively, such singularities should yield positive mass contributions. Gromov, in his four lectures[Gro23], has already proposed such questions for Alexandrov spaces. We prove PMT holds for low-dimensional singularities under the RCD condition, or when the Ricci curvature has a quadratic lower bound near the singular set (cf. Theorem1.3). Clarifying the sign contribution of different singularities will deepen our understanding of how local singular geometry affects global mass invariants on AH manifolds.
