## Abstract

We prove that ordinary Hilbert-Kunz multiplicity can be transcendental. More precisely, over every uncountable algebraically closed field of characteristic $p>2$, there exists a normal standard graded domain $S$ and an $S_+$-primary homogeneous ideal $I\subseteq S$ such that $e_{\rm HK}(IS_{S_+})$ is transcendental.

## 1.Introduction

Which real numbers occur as Hilbert–Kunz multiplicities? Even the existence of a transcendental value has remained open. We prove that such values occur.

Let(R,𝔪)(R,\mathfrak{m})be a Noetherian local ring of characteristicp>0p>0, and letI⊆RI\subseteq Rbe an𝔪\mathfrak{m}-primary ideal. ForQ=peQ=p^{e}, theQQth Frobenius power ofI=(f1,…,fs)I=(f_{1},\ldots,f_{s})isI[Q]=(f1Q,…,fsQ).I^{[Q]}=(f_{1}^{Q},\ldots,f_{s}^{Q}).

Monsky proved that the normalized lengthsλR​(R/I[Q])QdimR\frac{\lambda_{R}(R/I^{[Q]})}{Q^{\dim R}}

converge asQ→∞Q\to\infty[2]. Their limit is the Hilbert–Kunz multiplicityeHK​(I)=lime→∞λR​(R/I[pe])pe​dimR.e_{\rm HK}(I)=\lim_{e\to\infty}\frac{\lambda_{R}(R/I^{[p^{e}]})}{p^{e\dim R}}.

Hilbert–Kunz multiplicity is a numerical invariant of singularities in prime characteristic. It detects regularity in the unmixed case[3], is closely related to tight closure,FF-singularities, andFF-signature, and has geometric interpretations involving vector bundles, Frobenius pullbacks, Harder–Narasimhan filtrations, and density functions[4,5].

Rationality is known in several important settings. Brenner proved that the Hilbert–Kunz multiplicity of a homogeneousR+R_{+}-primary ideal is rational in a two-dimensional graded domain of finite type over an algebraically closed field[4]. Monsky later proposed a likely counterexample to rationality[6]and showed, conditional on precise conjectures for colengths associated with a nodal cubic in characteristic two, that algebraic irrational and transcendental values should occur[7,8]. Brenner constructed the first unconditional irrational example in[9]. A related transcendence result for epsilon multiplicity was recently obtained from divisor volumes[27]. The corresponding question for ordinary Hilbert–Kunz multiplicity remained open.

## 1.1.A transcendental generalized Hilbert–Kunz multiplicity

We first work with generalized Hilbert–Kunz multiplicity. For an idealJJin a local ring(R,𝔪)(R,\mathfrak{m}), setegHK​(J)=lime→∞λR​(H𝔪0​(R/J[pe]))pe​dimR,e_{\rm gHK}(J)=\lim_{e\to\infty}\frac{\lambda_{R}\bigl(H^{0}_{\mathfrak{m}}(R/J^{[p^{e}]})\bigr)}{p^{e\dim R}},

Dao–Watanabe computed it for several classes of rings[23], Brenner–Caminata treated the two dimensional graded case[24], and Asgharzadeh studied its relation with the LC condition and further curve cases[25]. Vraciu proved the reduction result used in Section5[31]. A broader treatment forpp-families of ideals appears in[26].
