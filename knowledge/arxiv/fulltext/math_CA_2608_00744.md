## Abstract

Making a progress in an Erdös problem dating back to 1957, we show that $$\max_{t \in {\Bbb R}}{|P_n(e^{it})|^2} \geq n+1 + \frac{1}{38}n^{1/3}$$ for every polynomial $P_n$ of degree $n$ with all of its coefficients in $\{-1,1\}$.

## Problem 1.1 (Littlewood's Flatness Problem)

The existence of an ultraflat sequence of unimodular polynomials seemed very unlikely, in view of a
1957 conjecture of P. Erdős (Problem 22 in [Er57]) asserting that, for allPn∈𝒦nP_{n}\in{\Cal{K}}_{n}withn≥1n\geq 1,maxt∈ℝ⁡|Pn​(ei​t)|≥(1+ε)​n+1,\max_{t\in{\mathbb{R}}}{|P_{n}(e^{it})|}\geq(1+\varepsilon)\sqrt{n+1}\,,1.3

Yet, refining a method of Körner [Kö80], Kahane [Ka80] proved that there exists
a sequence(Pn)(P_{n})withPn∈𝒦nP_{n}\in{\Cal{K}}_{n}which is(εn)(\varepsilon_{n})-ultraflat, whereεn=O​(n−1/17​log⁡n).\varepsilon_{n}=O\left(n^{-1/17}\sqrt{\log n}\right)\,.(Kahane’s paper contained though a slight error which was corrected in [QS96].)
Thus the Erdős conjecture (1.3) was disproved for the classes𝒦n{\Cal{K}}_{n}.
For the more restricted classℒn{\Cal{L}}_{n}the analogous Erdős conjecture
is unsettled to this date. It is a common belief that the analogous Erdős conjecture
forℒn{\Cal{L}}_{n}is true, and consequently there is no ultraflat sequence of polynomialsPn∈ℒnP_{n}\in{\Cal{L}}_{n}.
An interesting result related to Kahane’s breakthrough is given in [Be91].
For an account of some of the work done till the mid 1960’s, see Littlewood’s book [Li68]
and [QS96]. Littlewood polynomials with smallL4L_{4}norm have also
been intensively studied, see [BM00], [BC00], [BC01],[BC02], and [JK13], for example.

The structure of ultraflat sequences of unimodular polynomials is studied in

conjectures of Saffari [Sa92] and Queffelec and Saffari [QS96] are proved.
A recent paper of Bombieri and Bourgain [BB09] is devoted to the construction of ultraflat

A recent breakthrough result by Balister, Bollobás, Morris,
