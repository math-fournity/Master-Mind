## Abstract

de Brecht, Goubault-Larrecq, Jia and Lyu asked whether every sober convergence Choquet-complete space is domain-complete. We introduce the notion of singleton Choquet-completeness, a weakening of convergence Choquet-completeness in which the open sets chosen by player $α$ are required to have a singleton intersection, but not necessarily to form a neighbourhood basis. We prove that every singleton Choquet-complete $T_1$ space is domain-representable. Consequently, every convergence Choquet-complete $T_1$ space is domain-representable and hence sober. Thus, in the $T_1$ case, the sobriety assumption in the above question is redundant, and the question reduces to whether every convergence Choquet-complete $T_1$ space is domain-complete.

## 1Introduction

Domain theory provides a powerful order-theoretic framework for topology.
The Scott topology on a dcpo plays a central role in domain theory and theoretical
computer science, but it is, in general, onlyT0T_{0}. However, by considering the
spaceMax⁡(P)\operatorname{Max}(P)of maximal points of a posetPP, equipped with the
relative Scott topology, one can obtain allT1T_{1}spaces[17,18]. A
topological spaceXXis said to have aposet modelif it is homeomorphic
to such a maximal point space. In particular, spaces admitting a domain model are
calleddomain-representableby Bennett and Lutzer[2,3], whereas Martin refers to them as spaceshaving a
domain modelin several of his papers (e.g.,[13]). A classical example
is the poset𝕀​ℝ\mathbb{IR}of all bounded closed intervals of real numbers,
ordered by reverse inclusion, which is a bounded completeω\omega-domain model of the Euclidean spaceℝ\mathbb{R}.

Spaces with domain models have been extensively studied. Lawson proved that a
space is Polish if and only if it has anω\omega-domain model satisfying the
Lawson condition[11]. Martin[14]showed that the maximal point
spaceMax⁡(P)\operatorname{Max}(P)of anω\omega-continuous dcpoPPis regular if and
only if it is Polish. Formal balls of metric spaces, introduced by Weihrauch and
Schreiber as a domain-theoretic representation of metric spaces[16], were later used by Edalat and Heckmann to prove that every
complete metric space is domain-representable[6].

As Martin highlighted in[12], the property thatMax⁡(P)\operatorname{Max}(P)constitutes aGδG_{\delta}subset is of great utility across a wide range of applications. A typical example can be found in Edalat’s work[7], which explores the connections between measure theory and probabilistic powerdomains. His analysis hinges on the key assumption that each separable metric space can be embedded as aGδG_{\delta}-subspace within a countably based domain. Inspired by these application scenarios, Martin established two general results characterizing theGδG_{\delta}property of spaces of maximal elements:
- 1.

LetPPbe anω\omega-domain. IfMax⁡(P)\operatorname{Max}(P)is regular, thenMax⁡(P)\operatorname{Max}(P)is aGδG_{\delta}subset ofPP[14];
- 2.

AnyGδG_{\delta}subspace of a domain-representable space possesses an ideal domain model[12];
- 3.

AGδG_{\delta}subset of a Choquet complete space (particularly of a domain representable space) is Choquet complete[14,10].

More recently, de Brecht, Goubault-Larrecq, Jia, and Lyu[4]studied
domain-complete spaces and LCS-complete spaces. In particular, they proved
that every continuous valuation on aGδG_{\delta}-subset of a locally compact
sober space extends to a measure. They also
introduced the weaker notion ofconvergence Choquet-completeness, defined
via the strong Choquet game, and posed the following question.

## Question 1.1.

Is every sober convergence Choquet-complete space domain-complete?

The main purpose of this paper is to show that, forT1T_{1}spaces, the
sobriety assumption is unnecessary. To do this, we first introduce a
slight weakening of convergence Choquet-completeness, calledsingleton Choquet-completeness. Roughly speaking, this means that
playerα\alphahas a winning strategy in the strong Choquet game such
that, for every play following this strategy, the open sets chosen byα\alphahave a singleton intersection.

In fact, we prove the following slightly stronger result. For every
singleton Choquet-completeT1T_{1}spaceXX, we construct a posetPXP_{X}such thatXXis homeomorphic toMF⁡(PX)\operatorname{MF}(P_{X}), the space of
maximal filters onPXP_{X}. Our construction follows the approach to poset
spaces developed by Mummert and Stephan[15]: the basic
open sets ofXXare encoded as conditions, and the order records finite
extensions of plays compatible with a fixed singleton winning strategy
for Playerα\alpha.

Finally, we show that the filter poset(Filt⁡(PX),⊆)(\operatorname{Filt}(P_{X}),\subseteq)is an algebraic dcpo whose compact
elements are precisely the principal filters. Moreover, the topology onMF⁡(PX)\operatorname{MF}(P_{X})coincides with the subspace topology inherited from the
Scott topology on this algebraic dcpo. Consequently,XXadmits a domain model.
