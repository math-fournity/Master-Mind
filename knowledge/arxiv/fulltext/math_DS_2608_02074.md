## Abstract

In this work, we analyze a completely degenerate germ of a real vector field at the origin of $\mathbb{R}^3$. We construct a generic three-parameter unfolding of this germ and identify the associated local bifurcations. We prove that the phase portrait of the unfolded system contains a one-parameter family of heteroclinic orbits connecting two hyperbolic equilibrium points.

## 5.  Conclusion and Research Perspectives

In this study, we constructed a generic three-parameter unfolding of a germ of three-dimensional vector fields exhibiting a completely degenerate singularity at the origin. Our local analysis characterized the bifurcation diagram and demonstrated the existence of a one-parameter family of heteroclinic orbits connecting distinct equilibrium points. These results underscore the complexity of local dynamics that can emerge around such strongly degenerate singularities.

However, several fundamental questions remain open. The possible existence of homoclinic orbits, which could introduce more complex or chaotic dynamics, remains to be determined. Additionally, a complete classification of generic bifurcations in this context has not been established. A fine stratification of the parameter space according to bifurcation types would provide a more global understanding of dynamic transitions.

Future research directions include :
- —

Studying the robustness of detected structures against higher-order perturbations.
- —

Extending results to complex or non-autonomous frameworks.
- —

Global analysis of invariant manifolds and their interactions in an enlarged neighborhood of the singularity.

These avenues highlight the fertile research landscape surrounding completely degenerate singular germs, intersecting bifurcation theory, qualitative dynamics, and geometry of dynamical systems.
