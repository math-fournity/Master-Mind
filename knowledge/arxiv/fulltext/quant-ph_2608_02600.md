## Abstract

In this paper, we present a unified framework for proving lower bounds for estimating functionals of quantum states. We therefore resolve several open problems by establishing lower bounds that match known upper bounds: we show that it requires $\widetildeΩ(N^2)$ samples to estimate the Uhlmann fidelity, trace distance, and von Neumann entropy. Moreover, they immediately imply matching query lower bounds of $\widetildeΩ(N)$ by quantum sample-to-query lifting. These lower bounds imply the near-optimality of a dozen quantum algorithms since 2016.

## 1Introduction

The estimation of quantum functionals of quantum states is a fundamental task in quantum information theory and statistics.
There has been a lot of work in the literature.
In particular, functionals of great interest include the Uhlmann fidelityF​(ρ,σ)=tr⁡(σ​ρ​σ)\mathrm{F}\lparen\rho,\sigma\rparen=\operatorname{tr}\lparen\sqrt{\sigma}\rho\sqrt{\sigma}\rparen, trace distanceT​(ρ,σ)=12​tr⁡(|ρ−σ|)\mathrm{T}\lparen\rho,\sigma\rparen=\frac{1}{2}\operatorname{tr}\lparen\lvert\rho-\sigma\rvert\rparen, and von Neumann entropyS​(ρ)=−tr⁡(ρ​log⁡(ρ))\mathrm{S}\lparen\rho\rparen=-\operatorname{tr}\lparen\rho\log\lparen\rho\rparen\rparen.
Estimators for these particular quantum functionals are known, whose current best sample complexities areO​(d2)O\lparen d^{2}\rparenfordd-dimensional quantum states (e.g.,[1,2,12,10,7]). However, their prior best sample lower bounds are onlyΩ​(d)\Omega\lparen d\rparen(cf.[8]).
The sample complexity of estimating such still remains open.
It is widely open whether a sample lower bound ofΩ~​(d2)\widetilde{\Omega}\lparen d^{2}\rparencan be established, which is believed to be the case as in the classical counterpart, the estimation of the total variation distance and Shannon entropy has shown to be almost as hard as the estimation of the whole discrete distribution.

In this paper, we present a unified framework for proving lower bounds for quantum functional estimation.
Specifically, for a functionϕ\phi, we consider the quantum functionalℒϕ​(ρ)=1d​∑j=1dϕ​(d​λj​(ρ)),\mathcal{L}_{\phi}\lparen\rho\rparen=\frac{1}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{d}\phi\lparen d\lambda_{j}\lparen\rho\rparen\rparen,

whereλj​(ρ)\lambda_{j}\lparen\rho\rparendenotes thejj-th eigenvalue of thedd-dimensional quantum stateρ\rho.
In particular,ϕF​(x)=x\phi_{\mathrm{F}}\lparen x\rparen=\sqrt{x}recovers the Uhlmann fidelity,ϕT​(x)=|x−1|2\phi_{\mathrm{T}}\lparen x\rparen=\frac{\lvert x-1\rvert}{2}recovers the trace distance, andϕS​(x)=x​log⁡(x)\phi_{\mathrm{S}}\lparen x\rparen=x\log\lparen x\rparenrecovers the von Neumann entropy.
We end up with matching sample lower bounds for estimating the Uhlmann fidelity, trace distance, and von Neumann entropy.

## Theorem 1.1(Sample lower bounds for functional estimation).

Estimating the Uhlmann fidelityF​(ρ,σ)\mathrm{F}\lparen\rho,\sigma\rparen, trace distanceT​(ρ,σ)\mathrm{T}\lparen\rho,\sigma\rparen, and von Neumann entropyS​(ρ)\mathrm{S}\lparen\rho\rparenrequires sample complexityΩ~​(d2)\widetilde{\Omega}\lparen d^{2}\rparen.

More precisely, for additive errorε\varepsilon, the sample complexity lower bounds areΩ​(d2ε2​log4⁡(d))\Omega\left\lparen\frac{d^{2}}{\varepsilon^{2}\log^{4}\lparen d\rparen}\right\rparen

As a corollary, we can further show a matching sample complexity lower bound for spectrum estimation.

## Corollary 1.2(Sample lower bound for spectrum estimation).

Estimating the spectrum of an unknown quantum state requires sample complexityΩ~​(d2)\widetilde{\Omega}\lparen d^{2}\rparen.
More precisely, for precisionε\varepsilon(in the total variation distance to the spectrum), it requires sample complexityΩ~​(d2/ε2)\widetilde{\Omega}\lparen d^{2}/\varepsilon^{2}\rparen.
