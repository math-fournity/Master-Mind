## Abstract

In this paper, we establish a one-to-one correspondence between a natural monoid and the endomorphisms of the $2$-adic ring $C^*$-algebra $\mathcal{Q}_2$. As a consequence, we classify endomorphisms of $\mathcal{Q}_2$ with prescribed images and derive several criteria for the uniqueness of extensions of endomorphisms of a canonical copy of the Cuntz algebra $\mathcal{O}_2$ inside $\mathcal{Q}_2$ to endomorphisms of $\mathcal{Q}_2$. Moreover, we construct an example of an extendable automorphism of $\mathcal{O}_2$ that is not a composition of the flip-flop automorphism, the gauge automorphisms, and inner automorphisms, thereby providing negative answers to certain open questions. Using this explicit construction, we also show that the canonical image of $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal{Q}_2,\mathcal{O}_2)$ in the outer automorphism group $\operatorname{Out}(\mathcal{Q}_2)$ is non-abelian.   Finally, we characterize the automorphisms of $\mathcal{Q}_2$ that globally preserve $C^*(u)$, and completely describe the Weyl group $\mathcal{W}(\mathcal{Q}_2, C^*(u))$. Consequently, several related open questions are answered affirmatively.

## 1Introduction

Ever since Cuntz[15]introduced the Cuntz algebras𝒪n\mathcal{O}_{n}(n≥2n\geq 2), they have remained among the most important examples of infinite simpleC∗C^{*}-algebras and a rich source of intuition and counter-examples. Notably, Rosenberg[33]proved that𝒪n\mathcal{O}_{n}is an amenableC∗C^{*}-algebra that is not strongly amenable, thereby resolving a problem posed by Johnson[21, 10.2].

The Cuntz–Takesaki correspondence between the unitaries of the Cuntz algebra𝒪n\mathcal{O}_{n}and its endomorphisms generated substantial interest in the structure of its endomorphisms ([11,12,13,14,16]). Applications of endomorphisms of𝒪n\mathcal{O}_{n}can be found in quantum field theory[18]and the theory of wavelets[7]. Another important development in the study of automorphisms of𝒪n\mathcal{O}_{n}is the Weyl group associated with the Cartan inclusion𝒟n⊆𝒪n\mathcal{D}_{n}\subseteq\mathcal{O}_{n}(see[11,12]for example). Cuntz[16]showed that this Weyl group is discrete, and Conti and Szymański[14]proved that it is related to the Higman–Thompson group inside𝒪n\mathcal{O}_{n}. The restricted Weyl group is studied in[11].

One way to view the22-adic ringC∗C^{*}-algebra𝒬2\mathcal{Q}_{2}is as a symmetrized version of the Cuntz algebra𝒪2\mathcal{O}_{2}[25]. Its canonical isometries satisfy the Cuntz relations together with an intertwining relation that prevents many structural phenomena possible in𝒪2\mathcal{O}_{2}from occurring in𝒬2\mathcal{Q}_{2}. The canonical isometries generate a canonical copy of𝒪2\mathcal{O}_{2}inside𝒬2\mathcal{Q}_{2}, and there is no conditional expectation from𝒬2\mathcal{Q}_{2}onto this copy[2]. This suggests a strong rigidity phenomenon for the inclusion𝒪2⊆𝒬2\mathcal{O}_{2}\subseteq\mathcal{Q}_{2}.

Recently, Bassi and Conti[6]showed that the inclusion𝒪2⊆𝒬2\mathcal{O}_{2}\subseteq\mathcal{Q}_{2}is rigid, while in[30]we proved that there are no proper intermediateC∗C^{*}-subalgebras between𝒪2\mathcal{O}_{2}and𝒬2\mathcal{Q}_{2}. Earlier, Aiello, Conti, and Rossi studied permutative endomorphisms of𝒪2\mathcal{O}_{2}that extend to endomorphisms of𝒬2\mathcal{Q}_{2}[3]and characterized certain classes of endomorphisms that fix specified generators of𝒬2\mathcal{Q}_{2}[2].

This paper addresses several important questions concerning endomorphisms and a Weyl group of𝒬2\mathcal{Q}_{2}that were posed in[4].

Motivated by the Cuntz–Takesaki correspondence, we establish a one-to-one correspondence between a certain monoid and the endomorphisms of the22-adic ringC∗C^{*}-algebra𝒬2\mathcal{Q}_{2}. As a consequence, we obtain two classifications for endomorphisms of𝒬2\mathcal{Q}_{2}. One of them generalizes[2, Theorems 6.14 & 6.18].

## 4Uniqueness of extensions of endomorphisms of𝒪2\mathcal{O}_{2}

LetλV∈End⁡(𝒪2)\lambda_{V}\in\operatorname{End}(\mathcal{O}_{2})be an extendable endomorphism.
