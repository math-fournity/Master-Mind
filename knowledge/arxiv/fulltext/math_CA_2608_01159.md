## Abstract

We prove sharp weak and strong off-diagonal weighted inequalities for the positive Bergman operator on the upper half-plane when the parameter defining the operator is not necessarily the same as the one of the weighted Lebesgue spaces in consideration. The inequalities are obtained with the help of an adapted off-diagonal extrapolation result.
