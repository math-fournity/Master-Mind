# Linear relations modulo meager sets

**Published**: 2026-07-25

## Abstract

We prove several topological zero-one laws. First, we show that, if a subset $A$ of a Banach space $X$ has the Baire property, then $A$ admits a somewhere dense set of vectors $x \in X$ such that $A+x$ agrees with $A$ modulo meager sets if and only if it is either meager or comeager. Additional equivalent conditions are given if $X=\mathbb{R}$.   Second, we prove that if $A_1,\ldots,A_k\subseteq \mathbb{R}$ are subsets with the Baire property, $α_1,\ldots,α_k$ are nonzero reals with distinct finite sums, and $(x_{n,i}: n\in ω)$ are injective real sequences which converge to $0$ for each $i=1,\ldots,k$, then $$ \sum_{i=1}^k α_i(1_{A_i+x_{n,i}}-1_{A_i})=0 $$ modulo meager sets for all $n \in ω$ if and only if each $A_i$ is either meager or comeager. Finally, we provide some additional results in the case where the $α_1,\ldots,α_k$ do not have distinct finite sums. For instance, if $k=2$ and $α_1=α_2$, then the above equality holds modulo meager sets for all $n \in ω$ if and only if each $A_i$ is either meager or comeager or if $\{A_1,A_2\}$ is a partition of $\mathbb{R}$ modulo meager sets. Additional characterizations are given in the case $k\ge 3$. These results yield the category analogues of several results by Fejzić, Freiling, and Rinne in [J. London Math. Soc.~\textbf{82} (2010), no. 3, 717--732].

## 1.Introduction and Main results

It is known that a measurable setA⊆ℝA\subseteq\mathbb{R}has arbitrarily small periods (that is,A+xn=AA+x_{n}=Afor alln∈ωn\in\omega, where(xn)(x_{n})stands for a positive real sequence with limit0) if and only if eitherAAor its complement has measure0, see Exercise 3 in Rudin’s book[16, p.156]. (Note thatA+xn=AA+x_{n}=Acan be rewritten equivalently as𝟏A+xn−𝟏A=0\bm{1}_{A+x_{n}}-\bm{1}_{A}=0, where𝟏S\bm{1}_{S}stands for the characteristic function of a setSS.)

Motivated by this fact, Fast, Fejzić, Freiling, and Rinne studied in[7,9,8]the analogous situation of measurable setsA1,…,Ak⊆ℝA_{1},\ldots,A_{k}\subseteq\mathbb{R}which satisfy(1)∀n∈ω,∑i=1kαi​(𝟏Ai+xn,i−𝟏Ai)=0​a.e.,\forall n\in\omega,\quad\sum_{i=1}^{k}\alpha_{i}(\bm{1}_{A_{i}+x_{n,i}}-\bm{1}_{A_{i}})=0\,\,\,\text{ a.e.},

(that is, modulo sets of measure zero), whereα1,…,αk\alpha_{1},\ldots,\alpha_{k}are given nonzero reals, and(xn,i:n∈ω)(x_{n,i}:n\in\omega)is an injective real sequence with limit0for eachi=1,…,ki=1,\ldots,k.

In this manuscript, we provide several category analogues of the above results, thereby answering an open question in[9]. We divide our work into two main parts.
- (i)

In the first part, given a Banach spaceXX, we provide necessary and sufficient conditions on a setA⊆XA\subseteq Xwith the Baire property to admit a somewhere dense set of vectorsx∈Xx\in Xfor whichA+x=AA+x=Amodulo meager sets, see Theorem2.1. It turns out that this happens if and only if eitherAAor its complement is meager, see Theorem2.1. Additional equivalent conditions in the one-dimensional caseX=ℝX=\mathbb{R}can be found in Corollary2.4. These results can be seen as topological zero-one laws, in the same spirit of[13, Theorem 8.46], cf. also[2,3,4].
- (ii)

In the second part, we specialize to the caseX=ℝX=\mathbb{R}, and characterize the category analogue of (1) for setsA1,…,AkA_{1},\ldots,A_{k}with the Baire property. More precisely, we prove that if all finite sums of theαj\alpha_{j}are distinct, then each setAiA_{i}has to be either meager or comeager, see Theorem2.7. In addition, ifk=2k=2andα1=α2\alpha_{1}=\alpha_{2}, then at least one of the following two conditions holds: (a) eachAiA_{i}is either meager or comeager, or (b)A1=ℝ∖A2A_{1}=\mathbb{R}\setminus A_{2}modulo meager sets, see Theorem2.8. Additional results are given in the general casek≥3k\geq 3, see Theorem2.11and Proposition2.13.

We present our main results in Section2. Their proofs are given in Section4. We remark that our techniques are completely different from the measure counterparts given in[7,9,8].
