## Abstract

Building on work of Kassel--Loday and Zelmanov--Zhang, we show that a Steinberg Lie algebra of the complex group algebra of a (suitable) Bestvina--Brady group is of type $FP_2$ and is not finitely presented. This also allows us to answer the (homological) $0$-$1$-$2$ question for Lie algebras.

## 1.Introduction

Recall that for a nonnegative integernna complex Lie algebraLLis said to be (of type)F​PnFP_{n}if there exists an exact sequence of leftU​(L)\mathrm{U}(L)-modulesPn→…→P2→P1→P0→ℂ→0P_{n}\to\dots\to P_{2}\to P_{1}\to P_{0}\to\mathbb{C}\to 0

Every complex Lie algebra isF​P0FP_{0}, and it isF​P1FP_{1}if and only if it is finitely generated.
A finitely presented complex Lie algebra isF​P2FP_{2}, and the converse has been recorded as an open problem, for instance in[Kochloukova19,KochloukovaMartínez-Pérez22,Kochloukova26].

It follows from[BryantGroves99a,BryantGroves99b]that for metabelian Lie algebras overℂ\mathbb{C}finite presentability is equivalent toF​P2FP_{2}.
The same equivalence holds forℕ\mathbb{N}-graded Lie algebras by[Weigel15].

The celebrated[BestvinaBrady97]resolved the group theoretic analog producing groupsGGthat areF​P2FP_{2}overℂ\mathbb{C}yet not finitely presented.
OurF​P2FP_{2}complex Lie algebra that is not finitely presented is the Steinberg Lie algebra𝔰​𝔱n​(ℂ​G)\mathfrak{st}_{n}(\mathbb{C}G)withn=5n=5(other choices ofnnwould have worked as well, as would other base fields in place ofℂ\mathbb{C}).
Our proof builds on the finite presentability criterion for associative algebras of[PassmanSmall21], the finite presentability criterion for Steinberg Lie algebras of[ZelmanovZhang21](as well as its proof), the identification of𝔰​𝔱n\mathfrak{st}_{n}with the universal central extension of𝔰​𝔩n\mathfrak{sl}_{n}by[KasselLoday82], and a criterion for a Lie algebra to beF​P2FP_{2}from[KochloukovaMartínez-Pérez19].

An application is a counterexample to the prolongation ton=0n=0of the (homological)nn-n+1n+1-n+2n+2conjecture stated in[KochloukovaMartínez-Pérez19]forn≥1n\geq 1.
