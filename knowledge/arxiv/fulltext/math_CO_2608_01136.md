## Abstract

In recent work, the authors defined a sequence $(a_n)_{n\ge 0}$ to be $m$-convolutive exactly if   \begin{align*}   \sum_{n\ge 0} a_{mn} q^n = \left(\sum_{n\ge 0} a_n q^n\right)^m   \end{align*}   for a specific positive integer $m$ and provided proofs of the $2$- and $3$-convolutivity of a small number of sequences arising from primitive eta-products. Since the completion of that work, the authors have discovered many new instances of convolutive eta-products. The main focus of this work is to unify all but one of these instances in a parametric way.
