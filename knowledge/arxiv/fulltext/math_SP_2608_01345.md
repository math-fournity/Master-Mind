## Abstract

We show that for every closed, smooth manifold $(M,g)$ of dimension $d$, there exists $c(g)$ such that any nodal domain $Ω_λ$ of a Laplace eigenfunction with eigenvalue $λ$ contains a geodesic ball of radius at least $c(g) λ^{-1/2} \log\log(λ)^{-1/2}$ if $d=3$ and $c(g) λ^{-1/2} \log(λ)^{-\frac{d-3}{2}}$ if $d >3$. This ball is centered at any point at which the eigenfunction attains its maximum in absolute value within the nodal domain. Furthermore, we show that for any $d \geq 3$, there exist sequences of $λ$-nodal domains on $\mathbb{T}^d$ whose inner radius is of order $o(λ^{-1/2})$.
