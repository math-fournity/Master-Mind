# The double super Yangians in type A for arbitrary $0^m 1^n$-sequences and their bosonic representations

## Abstract

In this paper, we introduce the double super Yangian $\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})$ and $\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{sl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})$ associated with any fixed $0^{m}1^{n}$--sequence $\mathfrak{s}$. First, we establish an explicit isomorphism between the Drinfeld and R--matrix presentations of $\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{gl}^{\mathfrak{s}}_{m|n})$. We then generalize the notion of the quantum Berezinian to $\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})$, and employ it to construct the R--matrix presentation of $\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{sl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})$ and prove that it is isomorphic to the Drinfeld presentation. As an application, we present level--1 bosonic representations for $\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})$ and $\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{sl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})$ in terms of their Drinfeld current generators.

## 1Introduction

Quantum groups, introduced by Drinfeld[8]and Jimbo[19], have been extensively studied over the past few decades and now play important roles across mathematics and physics. A key example is the YangianY​(𝔤)\mathrm{Y}(\mathfrak{g}), a canonical deformation of the universal enveloping algebraU​(𝔤​[t])\mathrm{U}(\mathfrak{g}[t])for a finite-dimensional semisimple Lie algebra𝔤\mathfrak{g}. The general linear YangianY​(𝔤​𝔩n)\mathrm{Y}(\mathfrak{gl}_{n})was first given by Tarasov[36]for𝔤​𝔩2\mathfrak{gl}_{2}and later extended to the general case by Drinfeld[7]. It is a unital associative algebra that also admits a presentation in terms of defining relations written in a specific matrix form within the R–matrix formalism developed by Faddeev, Reshetikhin, and Takhtajan[15]. This alternative presentation endows the Yangian with a natural bialgebra structure, which can be extended to a Hopf algebra by adjoining an antipode.

In 1988, Drinfeld[9]proposed a new current realization for the Yangian, known as the Drinfeld new presentation, and further conjectured its equivalence to the R–matrix presentation without proof.
The conjecture was first confirmed by Brundan and Kleshchev[4]for type𝑨\boldsymbol{A}, using the Gauss decomposition of the generator matrixT​(u)T(u). In 2018, the equivalence of the two presentations for types𝑩,𝑪,𝑫\boldsymbol{B},\boldsymbol{C},\boldsymbol{D}was established in[20].

In the super case, Nazarov[32]defined the super Yangian associated with the Lie superalgebra𝔤​𝔩m|n\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}as a super analogue ofY​(𝔤​𝔩n)\mathrm{Y}(\mathfrak{gl}_{n})via the R–matrix presentation. Subsequently, Gow[17]obtained a Drinfeld-type presentation corresponding to a standard0101-sequence, and Peng[33]constructed parabolic presentations of the super YangianY​(𝔤​𝔩m|n𝔰)\mathrm{Y}(\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})for an arbitrary0101-sequence𝔰\mathfrak{s}. A further development came from Tsymbaliuk[37], who linked arbitrary Drinfeld super Yangians of type𝑨\boldsymbol{A}via odd reflections, which correspond to index permutations. Finite-dimensional irreducible representations ofY​(𝔤​𝔩m|n𝔰)\mathrm{Y}(\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})were described via these odd reflections[25,28]in 2022. In addition, the Drinfeld presentation for orthosymplectic Yangians was given in[29], building upon the R–matrix presentation introduced in[1].

The quantum double, obtained by pairing a quantum group with its Hopf dual[9], provides the conceptual basis for the universal R–matrix of quantum enveloping algebras[6]. This construction solves the quantum Yang–Baxter equation on every irreducible representation and drives advances in representation theory[13,11],qq-conformal field theory[14], and knot theory[34,35]. The double Yangian, defined as the quantum double of the YangianY​(𝔤)\mathrm{Y}(\mathfrak{g}), applies naturally to massive field theory. The case without central extension was first considered for𝔤=𝔰​𝔩2\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{sl}_{2}[23]. The general case for𝔤=𝔤​𝔩n,𝔰​𝔩n\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{gl}_{n},\mathfrak{sl}_{n}was subsequently investigated by Iohara[18]within the R–matrix formalism with central extensions, where he also derived the Drinfeld commutation relations and constructed representations. Recently, Jing and Yang[39]established the isomorphism between the R–matrix and Drinfeld presentations of the double Yangian in type𝑨\boldsymbol{A}and constructed the central elements at the critical level, while the analogous isomorphism for types𝑩,𝑪,𝑫\boldsymbol{B},\boldsymbol{C},\boldsymbol{D}was given in[21].

The super counterpart of the double Yangian was also formulated via the R-–matrix formalism, first introduced by Zhang[42]for the standard general linear super case. Very recently, the quantum Berezinian, the PBW theorem, and reflection algebras for the double Yangian of𝔤​𝔩m|n\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}associated with the standard0101-sequence were established in[2,3].

Vertex representations of quantum affine algebras of simply laced types were constructed by Frenkel and Jing in[12], while the corresponding vertex operator representations and Drinfeld realizations for simply laced quantum affine superalgebras have appeared in[41]. For double Yangians, level 1 bosonization and vertex operators for type𝑨\boldsymbol{A}were constructed in[18], higher-level bosonization ofDYh​(𝔰​𝔩n)\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{sl}_{n})was obtained in[10], and level 1 bosonization for types𝑩,𝑪,𝑫\boldsymbol{B},\boldsymbol{C},\boldsymbol{D}was established in[21]. Using the results of[18], Kozic constructed commutative operators for the double YangianDY​(𝔰​𝔩n)\mathrm{DY}(\mathfrak{sl}_{n})in[24].

In type𝑨\boldsymbol{A}, 01–sequences are commonly used to specify the parities of generators of (affine) Lie superalgebras, and distinct parity sequences correspond to different Dynkin diagrams. A distinctive feature of Lie superalgebras is that they admit multiple non-isomorphic Dynkin diagrams. The isomorphism problem for Lie superalgebras of finite or affine type associated with distinct Dynkin diagrams was resolved in[26]. Subsequently, the analogous questions for quantum finite/affine superalgebras and type𝑨\boldsymbol{A}super Yangians were addressed by Yamane[40]and Tsymbaliuk[37], respectively. Nevertheless, the corresponding problem for double super Yangians has not yet been investigated. In this paper, we study the type𝑨\boldsymbol{A}double super Yangians associated with arbitrary sequences𝔰\mathfrak{s}.

The main results of this paper are threefold. First, we define the double Yangian of the Lie superalgebras𝔤​𝔩m|n𝔰\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}}and𝔰​𝔩m|n𝔰\mathfrak{sl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}}associated with an arbitrary sequence𝔰\mathfrak{s}, i.e., double super YangiansDYh​(𝔤​𝔩m|n𝔰)\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})andDYh​(𝔰​𝔩m|n𝔰)\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{sl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}}), via the R–matrix presentation. Next, we present their Drinfeld presentations using the Gauss decomposition of the generator matrix and establish the isomorphism between the two constructions following the approach of[39]. Finally, we construct level 1 representations of these double super Yangians in terms of bosons. To this end, we construct a Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt type basis forDYh​(𝔤​𝔩m|n𝔰)\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})and prove that it specializes to the universal enveloping algebra of the affine Lie superalgebra𝔤​𝔩^m|n𝔰\widehat{\mathfrak{gl}}^{\mathfrak{s}}_{m|n}. These two key tools are necessary for proving the equivalence of the two presentations. We also generalize the quantum Berezinian forDYh​(𝔤​𝔩m|n𝔰)\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})from standard parity to arbitrary cases. Following the approach of[38], this quantum Berezinian can be used to define the R–matrix presentation ofDYh​(𝔰​𝔩m|n𝔰)\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{sl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}}).

This paper is organized as follows. Section 2 collects the notation and preliminaries used throughout the paper. In Section 3, the double super YangianDYh​(𝔤​𝔩m|n𝔰)\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})is defined via the R–matrix presentation, and its Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem is established. Section 4 is devoted to deriving the relations for the Gaussian generators. The isomorphism between the Drinfeld and R–matrix presentations ofDYh​(𝔤​𝔩m|n𝔰)\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})is established in Section 5. Section 6 provides the quantum Berezinian forDYh​(𝔤​𝔩m|n𝔰)\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}}). In Section 7, we introduce both the R–matrix and Drinfeld presentations ofDYh​(𝔰​𝔩m|n𝔰)\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{sl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})and prove the equivalence between them. Finally, Section 8 presents a bosonic construction of level 1 modules forDYh​(𝔤​𝔩m|n𝔰)\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{gl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}})andDYh​(𝔰​𝔩m|n𝔰)\mathrm{DY}_{h}(\mathfrak{sl}_{m|n}^{\mathfrak{s}}).
