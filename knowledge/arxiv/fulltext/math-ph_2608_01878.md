## Abstract

We present a novel approach to the superintegrability of collective Hamiltonians invariant under a Hamiltonian action of a connected semisimple compact Lie group, $G$, on a symplectic manifold, $M$. By exploiting a Hamiltonian torus action that goes back to Guillemin and Sternberg [GS,1983], we demonstrate that the functional dimensions of $\mathfrak{H} := \mathcal{J}^*(C^\infty(\mathfrak{g}^*)^G)$, where $\mathcal{J}: M \to \mathfrak{g}^*$ is the momentum map of the $G$ action, and its centralizer $\mathfrak{F}$ in $C^\infty(M)$ satisfy the equality $\mathrm{ddim}(\mathfrak{H}) + \mathrm{ddim}(\mathfrak{F}) = \mathrm{dim}(M)$. Together with a non-triviality condition, this ensures that the Abelian Poisson algebra $\mathfrak{H}\subset C^\infty(M)$ represents a superintegrable system, and it also follows that the momentum map of the GS torus action yields action variables for the system. Our work provides a new insight into collective superintegrability complementing earlier results of Bolsinov and Jovanović.

## 4.Discussion

We have shown that the Abelian Poisson algebraℌ\mathfrak{H}(1.5) engenders
a superintegrable system in the sense of definition1.1for every Hamiltonian action of a connected compact Lie groupGGassociated
with a simple Lie algebra.
It is easy to see that (after small notational modifications) everything works for reductive compact Lie groups as well.
Our proof relied on the Hamiltonian torus action (2.26) that directly gives
action variables for these collective superintegrable systems.
This is important since action variables are often useful for semi-classical quantization and also because
the momentum map (2.25) may induce action variables
for many interesting superintegrable systems that can be obtained by Hamiltonian reduction
from systems covered by our framework. For example, a family of spin Calogero–Sutherland models result
from reductions of cotangent bundles of compact symmetric spacesG/KG/Kby using the action ofKKinherited
from left-multiplications.
(See[6]that focused on the non-compact case.)
A part of the pertinent GS torus action is expected to descend to the
reduced phase space, leading to action variables of those spin many-body models.
We plan to elaborate this in a future publication.

Our work complements the earlier results obtained by Bolsinov and Jovanović in[3]without
mentioning the GS torus action.
In fact, it has been one of the aims of the present paper to shine a new light on the usefulness of this tool,
which can also be incorporated into the Hamiltonian reduction approach
to superintegrable systems based on Poisson–Lie groups
and quasi-Hamiltonian methods[5].

Let us end with a remark on a connection between Thimm’s trick[8,12,21]and superintegrability.
For this, consider a connected compact Lie groupGGwith Lie algebra𝔤\mathfrak{g}and let𝔤n<𝔤n−1<⋯<𝔤1:=𝔤\mathfrak{g}_{n}<\mathfrak{g}_{n-1}<\cdots<\mathfrak{g}_{1}:=\mathfrak{g}(4.1)

be a chain of subalgebras with associated connected subgroupsGi<G1:=GG_{i}<G_{1}:=Gfori=1,…,ni=1,\dots,n.
IfGGacts with momentum map𝒥\mathcal{J}on a connected symplectic manifoldMM, then also the subgroups
act with corresponding momentum maps𝒥i=πi∘𝒥\mathcal{J}_{i}=\pi_{i}\circ\mathcal{J}, whereπi:𝔤∗→𝔤i∗\pi_{i}:\mathfrak{g}^{*}\to\mathfrak{g}_{i}^{*}is the projection dual to the inclusion𝔤i→𝔤\mathfrak{g}_{i}\to\mathfrak{g}.
Taken together, the Abelian Poisson subalgebrasℌi:=𝒥i∗​(C∞​(𝔤i∗)Gi)\mathfrak{H}_{i}:=\mathcal{J}_{i}^{*}(C^{\infty}(\mathfrak{g}_{i}^{*})^{G_{i}})ofC∞​(M)C^{\infty}(M)generate a ‘big’ Abelian subalgebraℌ\mathfrak{H},
which was Thimm’s basic observation[21].
By combining Lie theoretic (connectedness) results presented in[12]with a natural generalization of our proof of Theorem3.2,
it is not difficult to see that the ‘big’ℌ\mathfrak{H}and its
centralizer𝔉\mathfrak{F}inC∞​(M)C^{\infty}(M)always satisfy the equalityddim​(ℌ)+ddim​(𝔉)=dim(M){\mathrm{ddim}}(\mathfrak{H})+{\mathrm{ddim}}(\mathfrak{F})=\dim(M).

Of course, action variables are provided by the momentum map of the associated GS torus action also in this case.
The ‘big’ℌ\mathfrak{H}represents a Liouville integrable system only in very rare, celebrated cases[8].
However, as a special case of a general connection between Liouville integrability and superintegrability[3,11],
in theC∞C^{\infty}category it can always be extended to a Liouville integrable system.
