## Abstract

Belle and Belle II experiments have collected $e^+e^-$ collision data with center-of-mass energies at or near the $Υ(4S)$ resonance. Using total $1.4\,\mathrm{ab}^{-1}$ combined dataset, we present new measurements of branching fractions for $Ξ_c^{0/+}$ and $Λ_c^+$ baryons, including several first observations. Additionally, we report the initial search for $CP$ violation in singly Cabibbo-suppressed three-body decays of charmed baryons, providing a test of $U$-spin symmetry.

## Abstract

Belle and Belle II experiments have collectede+​e−e^{+}e^{-}collision data with center-of-mass energies at or near theΥ​(4​S)\Upsilon(4S)resonance.
Using total1.4​ab−11.4\,\mathrm{ab}^{-1}combined dataset, we present new measurements of branching fractions forΞc0⁣/+\Xi_{c}^{0/+}andΛc+\Lambda_{c}^{+}baryons, including several first observations.

## 1Introduction

Charmed baryons provide a unique system for studying the dynamics of the interplay between strong and weak interactions.
Operating at the KEKB[Kurokawa:2001nw,Abe:2013kxa]and SuperKEKB[Akai:2018mbz]e+​e−e^{+}e^{-}colliders, Belle[Belle:2000cnh]and Belle II[Belle-II:2010dht]have collected a total of1.4​ab−11.4\text{ ab}^{-1}.
These experiments generally reconstruct charm baryons produced in the continuume+​e−→c​c¯e^{+}e^{-}\to c\bar{c}process.
The process offers a relatively clean experimental signature for the reconstruction of charm baryon particles.

These proceedings summarize recent results of charm baryon decays using the datasets from the Belle and Belle II (Run 1).
Specifically, we report new measurements of branching fractions and searches for rare decays in variousΞc0⁣/+\Xi_{c}^{0/+}andΛc+\Lambda_{c}^{+}decays.
Furthermore, we present first searches forC​PCPviolation in singly Cabibbo-suppressed three-body decays ofΞc+\Xi^{+}_{c}andΛc+\Lambda_{c}^{+}baryons.

## 2.1Ξc0→Ξ0​h​(h=π0,η,η′)\Xi_{c}^{0}\to\Xi^{0}h\;(h=\pi^{0},\eta,\eta^{\prime})

The preliminary results presented at this conference have since been published in[Belle:2025qrq].
These decays are classified as singly Cabibbo-suppressed (SCS) processes.

This analysis was performed using the combined datasets from Belle (988.4​fb−1988.4\text{ fb}^{-1}) and Belle II (427.9​fb−1427.9\text{ fb}^{-1}).

## 2.3Ξc+→p​KS0,Λ​π+,Σ0​π+\Xi_{c}^{+}\to pK_{S}^{0},\Lambda\pi^{+},\Sigma^{0}\pi^{+}

The study of SCSΞc+\Xi_{c}^{+}decays into a light baryon and a meson provides essential data to testSU​(3)F\mathrm{SU}(3)_{F}flavor symmetry and various topological diagrammatic approaches in the charmed baryon sector.
These signal decays are SCS processes, where the interplay between various internalWW-emission andWW-exchange diagrams is expected to lead to non-zero branching fractions.

This analysis[Belle:2024xcs]utilized data samples from both Belle (983.0​fb−1983.0\text{ fb}^{-1}) and Belle II (427.9​fb−1427.9\text{ fb}^{-1}).
The signal decays were reconstructed via the following decay chainsKS0→π+​π−,Λ→p​π−,K_{S}^{0}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-},\Lambda\to p\pi^{-},andΣ0→Λ​γ\Sigma^{0}\to\Lambda\gamma.
To determine absolute branching fractions, the yields were normalized to the normalization modeΞc+→Ξ−​π+​π+\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Xi^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{+}[Belle:2019bgi].

We report the first observation of these three decay modes.
The statistical significances of all decay channels exceed10​σ10\sigmain both Belle and Belle II data, with the exception of theΞc+→Λ​π+\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda\pi^{+}mode in the Belle dataset, which has a significance of7.6​σ7.6\sigma.
For thep​KS0pK_{S}^{0}andΛ​π+\Lambda\pi^{+}mode, the dominant systematic uncertainties originate from the fitting method and Dalitz efficiency corrections.
In theΣ0​π+\Sigma^{0}\pi^{+}mode, the fitting uncertainty is the leading source, followed by comparable contributions fromγ\gammareconstruction and Dalitz efficiency corrections.

These results show varying degrees of agreement and tension with theoretical predictions, includingSU​(3)F\mathrm{SU}(3)_{F}flavor symmetry models[Zhong:2022exp,Hsiao:2021nsc]and topological diagrammatic or irreducibleSU​(3)\mathrm{SU}(3)approaches[Zhong:2024qqs].

## 2.5Λc+→p​KS0​π0\Lambda_{c}^{+}\to pK_{S}^{0}\pi^{0}

## 4Summary

Utilizing Belle and Belle II (Run 1) data, we have presented first observations and improved measurements of severalΞc0⁣/+\Xi_{c}^{0/+}andΛc+\Lambda_{c}^{+}decay modes.
These results provide vital constraints forSU​(3)F\mathrm{SU}(3)_{F}, nonfactorizable and other models.
Searches for CP violation in three-body charm baryon decays show no evidence of asymmetry, consistent withUU-spin symmetry predictions.
As Belle II Run 2 data collection continues, the targeted1​ab−11~\text{ab}^{-1}dataset in 2026 will enable even more stringent tests ofC​PCPviolation and the observation of rare decay modes that are currently statistically limited.
