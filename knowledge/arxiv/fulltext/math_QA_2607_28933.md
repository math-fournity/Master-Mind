## Abstract

A vertex operator algebra $V_L$ associated with a positive definite even lattice $L$ has a standard integral form, which we denote it by $V_{L,\mathbb{Z}} $. If $F$ is a field of characteristic $p>0$, it is known that $V_{L,F}:= F\otimes_\mathbb{Z} V_{L,\mathbb{Z}}$, a vertex algebra over $F$, is simple if and only if $(p, \det(L))=1$.   In this article, we study $V_{L,F}$ when the characteristic of $F$ divides $\det(L)$. We determine the radical $\mathrm{Rad}$ of the invariant bilinear form on $V_{L,F}$, show that it is the unique maximal ideal and study the quotient vertex algebra $V_{L,F}/\mathrm{Rad}$.
