## Abstract

The fundamental groups of compact 3-manifolds are known to be residually finite. Feng Luo conjectured that a stronger statement is true, by only allowing finite groups of the form $\mathrm{PGL}(2,R)$, where $R$ is a finite commutative ring. In earlier work, this conjecture was disproven in full generality. The conjecture arose in the context of orientable connected compact 3-manifolds which are geometrizable. By constructing explicit faithful linear representations using rings with nilpotent elements, we demonstrate that the conjecture holds for six of the eight Thurston model geometries, namely all but $\mathbb{S}^3$ and $\widetilde{\mathrm{SL}_2}$. In the case of $\mathbb{S}^3$, the conjecture holds if we replace $\mathrm{PGL}(2,R)$ with $\mathrm{GL}(2,R)$. A spherical counterexample for the projective variant is the Poincaré homology sphere $Σ(2,3,5)$. In the case of $\widetilde{\mathrm{SL}_2}$, the conjecture fails to hold for both the projective and non-projective variants; a counterxample is provided by the Brieskorn sphere $Σ(2,3,7)$.

## 1Introduction

In this paper, a33-manifold is a smooth33-manifold, and a geometric structure on a33-manifold, in the sense of Thurston, is a Riemannian metric onMMwhich is locally isometric to one of Thurston’s eight three-dimensional geometries:𝔼3,𝕊3,ℍ3,𝕊2×𝔼,ℍ2×𝔼,Nil,Sol,SL2~\mathbb{E}^{3},\mathbb{S}^{3},\mathbb{H}^{3},\mathbb{S}^{2}\times\mathbb{E},\mathbb{H}^{2}\times\mathbb{E},\mathrm{Nil},\mathrm{Sol},\widetilde{\mathrm{SL}_{2}}. Given an orientable connected compact33-manifoldMM, a hyperbolic structure onMMgives rise to a developing mapdev:M~→ℍ3\mathrm{dev}\colon\widetilde{M}\to\mathbb{H}^{3}

which is an isometry from the universal cover ofMMto three-dimensional hyperbolic space, and also a holonomy representationhol:π1​M↪Isom+​(ℍ3)\mathrm{hol}\colon\pi_{1}M\hookrightarrow\mathrm{Isom}^{+}(\mathbb{H}^{3})

which is an embedding from the fundamental group ofMMto the orientation-preserving isometry group of three-dimensional hyperbolic space. For details, see, e.g.,[Thu97],[Rat19]or[Mar16]. Noting thatIsom+​(ℍ3)≅PSL​(2,ℂ)\mathrm{Isom}^{+}(\mathbb{H}^{3})\cong\mathrm{PSL}(2,\mathbb{C}), this gives us an embedding of the fundamental group into a two-dimensional linear group. In the case thatMMis triangulated, the decomposition ofMMinto simplices lifts to one ofM~\widetilde{M}, which gives rise to a labelling of the0-skeleton of the lifted triangulation by elements of∂ℍ3=ℂ​P1\partial\mathbb{H}^{3}=\mathbb{C}P^{1}and this labelling encodes all of the information necessary to construct the holonomy representation; see[LTY13]for details. In[Luo16], Luo generalized these labellings to labellings overP1​(R)\mathrm{P}^{1}(R), the projective line over an arbitrary commutative ringRR, and constructed representations intoPGL​(2,R)\mathrm{PGL}(2,R)by solving Thurston’s hyperbolic gluing equations overRR. See[FGT18]for an example illustrating the strength of Luo’s generalization. Thus, for triangulated connected compact33-manifoldsMM, Luo is able to construct an ample supply of two-dimensional linear representations, over commutative rings, ofπ1​M\pi_{1}M. Based on this, Luo makes the following conjecture.

## Conjecture 1.1([Luo16]).

IfMMis a connected compact33–manifold andγ∈π1​M∖{1}\gamma\in\pi_{1}M\setminus\{1\}, then there exists a finite commutative ringRRand a homomorphismπ1​M→PGL​(2,R)\pi_{1}M\to\mathrm{PGL}(2,R)whose kernel does not containγ\gamma.

It is known that the fundamental groups of compact33-manifolds are residually finite; were this conjecture true, it would provide a concrete list of finite groups into which one could map without killing a given element. However, in[FGT18], we showed that the conjecture is false. In particular, we demonstrated that there exists a closed graph33-manifold which does not satisfy the conjecture. On the other hand, the conjecture arose in the context of orientable connected compact geometric33-manifolds, and so, in this paper we investigate the conjecture for these33-manifolds. We show that the conjecture in fact fails even in this case, but is true for six of the eight Thurston model geometries, namely all but𝕊3\mathbb{S}^{3}andSL2~\widetilde{\mathrm{SL}_{2}}. In the case of𝕊3\mathbb{S}^{3}, the situation is recoverable in the sense that the conjecture holds true, in a slightly weaker form (see Proposition2.3), if we replace projective general linear groups with special linear groups. On the other hand, in the case ofSL2~\widetilde{\mathrm{SL}_{2}}, the conjecture does not hold true even in this weaker form, or even with special linear groups replaced by general linear groups.


In fact, again by Proposition2.3, a finitely generated group satisfies the residual property referred to in the conjecture, withPGL\mathrm{PGL}replaced bySL\mathrm{SL}, if and only if it embeds intoSL​(2,R)\mathrm{SL}(2,R)for some, not necessarily finite, commutative ringRR. Thus, the main result of the paper is the following.

## Theorem 1.2.

IfMMis an orientable connected compact33-manifold which admits a geometric structure modelled on any one of Thurston’s eight model geometries, exceptSL2~\widetilde{\mathrm{SL}_{2}}, thenπ1​M\pi_{1}Membeds intoSL​(2,R)\mathrm{SL}(2,R)for some commutative ringRR; equivalently, given anyγ∈π1​M∖{1}\gamma\in\pi_{1}M\smallsetminus\{1\}, there exists a homomorphismπ1​M→SL​(2,Rfin)\pi_{1}M\to\mathrm{SL}(2,R_{\emph{fin}}), for some finite commutative ringRfinR_{\emph{fin}}, whose kernel does not containγ\gamma. In the case ofSL2~\widetilde{\mathrm{SL}_{2}}, a counterexample is provided by the Brieskorn sphereΣ​(2,3,7)\Sigma(2,3,7).
