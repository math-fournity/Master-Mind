## Abstract

We establish a Fenchel-Willmore-Chen inequality for smooth metric measure spaces with a lower bound on the 1-Bakry-Emery k-Ricci curvature. This extends previous results and yields a unified proof. In addition, a Sobolev and isoperimetric inequality for nonnegative 1-weighted Ricci curvature is proven.

The Fenchel–Willmore–Chen inequality establishes the sharp scale-invariant lower bound for the total mean curvature of a compact submanifoldMnM^{n}immersed inℝn+m.\mathbb{R}^{n+m}.It connects to several other geometric inequalities and plays an important role in the study of submanifolds. The classical Fenchel theorem[8]says for a closed space curve with curvatureκ\kappa,∫γκ​(s)​𝑑s≥2​π\int_{\gamma}\kappa(s)\,ds\geq 2\pi

with equality only for convex planar curves.

For a closed compact surfaceΣ\Sigmainℝ3\mathbb{R}^{3}with mean curvatureH=λ1+λ22H=\tfrac{\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}}{2}, Willmore[19]proved that∫ΣH2​𝑑A≥4​π\int_{\Sigma}H^{2}\,dA\geq 4\pi

with equality if and only ifΣ\Sigmais a round sphere. The left-hand side is referred to as the Willmore energy which is conformal invariant, plays an important role in elasticity of membranes, string/surface models in physics.

In 1971 Bang-Yen Chen[6]generalized it to submanifolds of any dimension and codimension.
For a closed compact submanifoldΣn\Sigma^{n}immersed inℝn+m\mathbb{R}^{n+m}, letH→=1n​tr​I​I\vec{H}=\tfrac{1}{n}\mbox{tr}IIbe the mean curvature vector:(0.1)∫Σ|H→|n​𝑑V≥vol​(𝕊n)\int_{\Sigma}|\vec{H}|^{n}\,dV\geq\mbox{vol}({\mathbb{S}}^{n})

with equality (forn>1n>1) if and only ifΣn\Sigma^{n}is an umbilical hypersphere inℝn+1\mathbb{R}^{n+1}.

Recently, there have been many extensions to much more general ambient spaces with lower-curvature bounds. Agostiniani, Fogagnolo, and Mazzieri[1]extended to compact hypersurfaces in manifolds with nonnegative Ricci curvature using a monotonicity
formula arising from potential theory. As pointed out in[3]the result also follows from Heintze-Karcher’s[10]comparison estimate. See also the proof by Wang[18], Brendle[3, Theorem 6.1]along this line. Ji-Kwong[11], Pan-Yi[14]extended to higher codimension for nonnegativekk-Ricci curvature. Other extensions include compact hypersurfaces in negative Ricci curvature lower bound[12], asymptotically nonnegative Ricci curvature[16], weighted curvature case[20,17], notably the substatic case[2]. The first named author[13]also extended to asymptotically negative and integral Ricci curvature case.

In this paper we generalize Fenchel–Willmore–Chen inequality to smooth metric
measure spaces with a lower bound on the 1-Bakry–Emerykk-Ricci curvature, recovering almost of all previous work.
