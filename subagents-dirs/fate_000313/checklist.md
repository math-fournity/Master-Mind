- 题目原文（数学描述）：Prove that R = k[s⁴, s³t, st³, t⁴] ⊂ k[s,t] (homogeneous coordinate ring of a smooth rational quartic in P³) is not Cohen-Macaulay.
- 解答核心思路（1-2句话）：Compute the Hilbert function by counting semigroup monomials; the h-vector (second differences) has h(3)=-1<0, impossible for a CM ring of dimension 2. The gap monomial s²t²∉R is the root cause.
- 解答关键步骤列表：
  1. R is a 2-dimensional graded domain (coordinate ring of a curve)
  2. Compute Hilbert function: H(0)=1, H(1)=4, H(n)=4n+1 for n≥2 (gap at (2,2))
  3. Compute h-vector (second differences): h(0)=1, h(1)=2, h(2)=2, h(3)=-1
  4. CM rings of dim 2 must have non-negative h-vectors → contradiction
  5. Root cause: s²t²∉R (the semigroup gap)
