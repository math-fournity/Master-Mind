- 题目原文（数学描述）：Let A = ℝ[X,Y]/(X²+Y²+1). Then A is a principal ideal domain.
- 解答核心思路（1-2句话）：View A as a quadratic extension ℝ[X][Y]/(Y²+X²+1) of the PID ℝ[X], show it's a Dedekind domain, then prove every prime ideal is principal by case analysis on the contraction to ℝ[X]. The key case (irreducible quadratic f≠X²+1) requires solving the norm equation a²+b²(X²+1)=f(X), whose solvability over ℝ follows from the irreducibility condition b²-4c<0.
- 解答关键步骤列表：
  1. A = ℝ[X][Y]/(Y²+X²+1) is a free ℝ[X]-module of rank 2, integral closure of PID ℝ[X] in K=ℝ(X)(√(-(X²+1))), hence a Dedekind domain
  2. Classify prime ideals of A by contraction to ℝ[X]: (0), (X-a) for a∈ℝ, (X²+1), and (f) for other irreducible quadratics
  3. Case (X-a): A/(X-a) = ℝ[Y]/(Y²+a²+1), irreducible since a²+1>0, so (X-a) stays prime in A — principal
  4. Case (X²+1): In A, X²+1 = -Y², so (X²+1) = (Y)², prime (Y) is principal
  5. Case f=X²+bX+c, b²-4c<0, f≠X²+1: A/(f) ≅ ℂ[Y]/(Y²+α²+1) splits into two primes; solve norm equation N(a+bY)=a²+b²(X²+1)=f(X) with deg(a)≤1, deg(b)=0; system a₁²+b₀²=1, 2a₁a₀=b, a₀²+b₀²=c has solution because b²-4c<0 ⟹ 4c>b² ⟹ discriminant (c-1)²+b² > 0 and t₋=[(c+1)-√((c-1)²+b²)]/2 ∈ [0,1]
  6. Since A is Dedekind and every prime is principal, every ideal (product of primes) is principal, so A is a PID
